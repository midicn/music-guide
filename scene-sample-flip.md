::: zh
采样作品能不能发，不取决于你改得多狠，取决于你清了几层权。**文件能下载，不等于能用。**

## 你要交的是什么

一首用了别人录音片段的新作品，加一份采样清单：来源、时长、许可状态。清单不是形式，是发布前唯一能救你的东西。

## 三步走

1. 查清来源与许可：作品权与录音权是两层，免费音源也要看条款。
2. 决定改造方式：切片、变调、变速、重排，改到一个片段不再承担整段伴奏。
3. 把采样与自己的演奏分层混，让采样是素材，不是底轨。

## 会用到的页面与工具

- [采样版权与清理](https://prod.midicn.com/sampling-copyright-and-clearance/) —— 两层权怎么分，文件许可为什么不够。
- [采样器结构](https://prod.midicn.com/sampler-architecture/) —— 样本池、映射、声部与包络，切片才切得准。
- [AI 采样生成](https://ai.midicn.com/sample-generation/) —— 生成 one-shot 后，长度、音高、尾巴与权利都要重核。
- [采样与变形](../culture/sampling-and-transform/) —— 采样在音乐史里一直是创作手法，不只是借用。
- [量化](https://prod.midicn.com/quantize/) —— 切片对不上拍时，吸多少格是个音乐决定。

## 验收标准

- 每个采样都能写明来源与许可状态，无法确认的片段不上发行版。
- 切片处无爆音，变调后共振峰与音色不塌，单声道检查不抵消。
- 采样不独占整首伴奏，作品里能听出你自己演奏或编排的部分。

::: en
Whether a sample-based track can be released depends less on how hard you flipped it than on how many rights you cleared. **A file you can download is not a file you can use.**

## What you are delivering

A new work built on someone else's recording, plus a sample sheet: source, length, licence status. The sheet is not paperwork; it is the only thing that saves you at release.

## Three steps

1. Trace source and licence: composition and recording are two layers, and even free libraries have terms.
2. Decide the transformation — chop, pitch, stretch, reorder — until no single fragment carries a whole backing.
3. Mix the sample and your own playing as separate layers, so the sample is material rather than the bed.

## Pages and tools to use

- [Sampling copyright and clearance](https://prod.midicn.com/sampling-copyright-and-clearance/) — the two layers, and why a file licence is not a work licence.
- [Sampler architecture](https://prod.midicn.com/sampler-architecture/) — pool, map, voices and envelopes; that is what makes a clean chop.
- [AI sample generation](https://ai.midicn.com/sample-generation/) — after generating one-shots, recheck length, tuning, tail and rights.
- [Sampling and transformation](../culture/sampling-and-transform/) — sampling has long been a compositional method, not just borrowing.
- [Quantize](https://prod.midicn.com/quantize/) — how far to pull a chop onto the grid is a musical decision.

## Acceptance checks

- Every sample has a stated source and licence status; anything unconfirmed stays off the release.
- Chops have no clicks, pitch shifts keep formants intact, and a mono check does not cancel.
- The sample does not own the whole backing; your own playing or arrangement is audible.
