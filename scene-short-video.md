短视频的背景乐没有"铺垫"的时间。观众拇指一动，你的前奏就没了。

## 成品长什么样

15 到 60 秒的一段音乐，能垫住口播或画面，**前两秒有钩子，结尾能自然回到开头**。它是一个可循环的片段，不是一首被剪短的歌。

## 分三步做

1. 先按分镜卡点：每个镜头切换处给一个音头或一次呼吸，别让切换落在乐句中间。
2. 把钩子提到最前，宁可牺牲前奏，也别让观众在第三秒才听到重点。
3. 按平台目标压响度，手机外放要清楚，别用母带级响度硬顶。

## 用到的页面

- [短视频音乐](https://ai.midicn.com/music-for-short-form/) —— 前两秒无钩子就没有后面，结构还要能循环。
- [钩子设计](https://prod.midicn.com/hook-design/) —— 短、可重复、可变形，三次才站得住。
- [平台响度目标](https://prod.midicn.com/platform-loudness-targets/) —— 各平台归一化方式不同，先查再压。
- [响度标准](https://prod.midicn.com/loudness-standards/) —— LUFS 与真峰值怎么读，别只看波峰。
- [背景音乐的利与弊](../culture/background-music/) —— 有口播时，中频要让位给语音。

## 什么算合格

- 前 2 秒内出现可辨识的钩子或明确节奏型。
- 循环点无爆音，首尾电平差不超过 0.5 dB。
- 响度约 −14 LUFS，真峰值 ≤ −1 dBTP，手机外放人声仍清楚。

::: en
Short video gives music no time to set up. One thumb movement and your intro is gone.

## What the finished piece looks like

Fifteen to sixty seconds that can sit under speech or picture, **with a hook in the first two seconds and an ending that returns naturally to the start**. It is a loopable fragment, not a song cut short.

## Do it in three steps

1. Cut to the shot list: give every cut a downbeat or a breath, and never let a cut land mid-phrase.
2. Move the hook to the front; sacrifice the intro rather than making viewers wait until second three.
3. Master to the platform target so phone speakers stay intelligible, instead of pushing release-level loudness.

## The pages you need

- [Music for short form](https://ai.midicn.com/music-for-short-form/) — no hook in two seconds means no viewer, and the loop must survive repeats.
- [Hook design](https://prod.midicn.com/hook-design/) — short, repeatable, transformable; three appearances to stick.
- [Platform loudness targets](https://prod.midicn.com/platform-loudness-targets/) — normalisation differs per platform; check before you push.
- [Loudness standards](https://prod.midicn.com/loudness-standards/) — how to read LUFS and true peak, not just sample peaks.
- [Background music, pros and cons](../culture/background-music/) — with voice-over, the midrange has to yield.

## What counts as good

- A recognisable hook or clear rhythmic figure appears within the first two seconds.
- The loop point has no click, and start and end levels differ by no more than 0.5 dB.
- Around −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, and speech stays clear on a phone speaker.
