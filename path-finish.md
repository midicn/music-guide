::: zh
「素材已经有了，问题在最后一步：噪音、平衡、响度、格式。」这条路处理的是「做完」到「能交」之间的距离，判断比插件重要。

## 这条路径适合谁

适合手上已有录音、分轨或生成音频的人；也适合作品被说「听着不像成品」的人。不适合歌还没写完的人——先回[写一首歌](../path-write/)。

## 先读这三页

- [混音导论](https://prod.midicn.com/mixing-introduction/) —— 混音解决「谁在何时被听见」，不是把每件乐器都做好听。
- [参考曲目工作法](https://prod.midicn.com/reference-track-method/) —— 用熟悉的成品当标尺，替代「今天耳朵准不准」。
- [响度标准与真峰值](https://prod.midicn.com/loudness-standards/) —— 交付的硬指标，先知道目标再动手。

## 接下来

- [音频修复](https://prod.midicn.com/audio-restoration/) —— 降噪、去嗡、去咔嗒各有代价。
- [混音顺序](https://prod.midicn.com/mixing-order/) —— 先平衡后处理，顺序对了每步都省力。
- [常见错误的听辨](https://prod.midicn.com/common-mix-mistakes/) —— 四种症状对应四类动作。
- [母带是什么](https://prod.midicn.com/what-is-mastering/) —— 混音之后最后一道加工。
- [母带交付格式](https://prod.midicn.com/master-delivery-formats/) —— 格式、采样率、位深与余量。
- [Stems 交付](https://prod.midicn.com/stems-delivery/) —— 按约定把混音拆成若干组音频。

## 可以跳过

作曲、编曲与配器；AI 生成与提示词；录音棚声学（除非你要进棚）。修、混、母带三件事本身就够学一季。

## 常见走弯路

1. 先挂母带插件。母带救不了混音里的频率打架。
2. 每轨都加 EQ 和压缩。动得越多，越听不出问题在哪。
3. 只在耳机上判断，或者把响度当成质量。换三套系统再决定。

The material exists; the problem is the last step: noise, balance, loudness, format. This path covers the distance between "finished" and "delivered", where judgement matters more than plug-ins.

## Who this path is for

For anyone with recordings, stems or generated audio in hand; also for anyone whose work gets called "not quite a finished record". Not for unfinished songs — go back to [Writing a Song](../path-write/).

## Read these three first

- [Introduction to mixing](https://prod.midicn.com/mixing-introduction/) — mixing answers who is heard when, not how to make every instrument sound nice.
- [The reference track method](https://prod.midicn.com/reference-track-method/) — use a finished record as the ruler instead of today's ears.
- [Loudness standards and true peak](https://prod.midicn.com/loudness-standards/) — the hard numbers of delivery; know the target before you touch anything.

## What comes next

- [Audio restoration](https://prod.midicn.com/audio-restoration/) — noise, hum and clicks each cost something to remove.
- [Mixing order](https://prod.midicn.com/mixing-order/) — balance before processing; the right order makes every step cheaper.
- [Hearing common mistakes](https://prod.midicn.com/common-mix-mistakes/) — four symptoms, four kinds of move.
- [What mastering is](https://prod.midicn.com/what-is-mastering/) — the last pass after the mix.
- [Master delivery formats](https://prod.midicn.com/master-delivery-formats/) — format, sample rate, bit depth and headroom.
- [Stems delivery](https://prod.midicn.com/stems-delivery/) — splitting a mix into agreed groups.

## What you can skip

Composition, arrangement and orchestration; AI generation and prompting; studio acoustics unless you are recording in one. Repair, mix and master alone will keep you busy for a season.

## Common detours

1. Reaching for a mastering plug-in first. Mastering cannot fix frequencies fighting in the mix.
2. EQ and compression on every track. The more you move, the less you can hear what is wrong.
3. Judging only on headphones, or treating loudness as quality. Check on three systems before deciding.
