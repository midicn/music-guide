学校作业丢分的地方，通常不是写得不够好，而是**格式不对、结构不完整、和题目要求对不上**。

## 最后拿到什么

按题目要求的一份成品：可能是谱面、录音、分析报告，或三者组合。先确认交付形式与评分点，再决定写什么。

## 分三步做

1. 把要求抄成一张清单：曲式、长度、配器、必须出现的元素、提交格式。
2. 按评分点搭结构：先定调性与段落，再写旋律，最后检查终止式与和声是否站得住。
3. 按格式导出并自检一遍：小节数、拍号、谱面与音频是否逐小节对得上。

## 什么算合格

- 小节总数、拍号与调号前后一致，无越界或漏拍的音符。
- 音频与谱面逐小节对得上，速度标记与实际演奏一致。
- 文件格式与命名符合老师要求（PDF / MP3 / WAV），附带一段 100 字以内的创作说明。

::: en
School projects usually lose marks for **wrong format, incomplete structure or missing the brief**, not for lack of invention.

## What you end up with

Whatever the assignment asks for: a score, a recording, an analysis, or a combination. Confirm the format and the marking criteria before deciding what to write.

## Do it in three steps

1. Turn the brief into a checklist: form, length, instrumentation, required elements, submission format.
2. Build to the marks: key and sections first, melody second, then check that cadences and harmony hold up.
3. Export in the required format and self-check: bar count, time signature, and whether score and audio match bar for bar.

- [Notation software introduction](https://prod.midicn.com/notation-software-introduction/) — a score is an editable structure; check layout and slurs before export.

- [Score delivery and stems](https://prod.midicn.com/score-delivery-and-stems/) — handing in a score is more than one file; naming and format count.

## What counts as good

- Bar count, time signature and key signature agree throughout, with no stray or missing beats.
- Audio matches the score bar for bar, and the tempo marking matches the performance.
- File format and naming follow the teacher's requirement (PDF / MP3 / WAV), with a note under 100 words.
