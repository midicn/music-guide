::: zh
排练最容易白干的原因：所有人都记得"昨天那版更好"，但没有人有录音。**没录下来的排练，等于没发生。**

## 你要交的是什么

一次排练的产物：每首歌一条完整参考录音、一份改了什么的清单、以及能交给别人的分轨或混音。不是"下周再来一次"。

## 三步走

1. 排练前定好速度、调性、段落顺序和谁数拍，把这些写在一张纸上。
2. 排练时整段录，别只录片段；一条完整的、有错的录音比十条碎片有用。
3. 当天导出，按"歌曲-乐器-日期"命名，分轨同起点、同长度，再写三行下次要改的。

## 会用到的页面与工具

- [排练准备清单](https://prod.midicn.com/session-prep-checklist/) —— 能提前决定的事全部提前，到场只演奏。
- [分轨录音](https://prod.midicn.com/multitrack-recording/) —— 隔离与余量比轨数重要。
- [协作与交接](https://prod.midicn.com/collaboration-and-handoff/) —— 让别人打开工程前，先让它能自己说明白。
- [分轨交付](https://prod.midicn.com/stems-delivery/) —— 按约定分组、命名，鼓和贝斯别混在一轨。
- [舞台与监听](../culture/stage-and-monitors/) —— 听不清自己时，排练效率掉的比你想的快。

## 验收标准

- 每首歌都有一条完整参考，时长与结构能听清主歌、副歌、solo。
- 分轨命名含歌曲、乐器与日期，全部同起点、同采样率、同长度。
- 排练后 24 小时内发出文件与改动清单，下一次排练前有人确认收到。

::: en
The usual way a rehearsal is wasted: everyone remembers "yesterday's take was better" and nobody recorded it. **A rehearsal that was not captured did not happen.**

## What you are delivering

The output of one session: a complete reference recording per song, a list of what changed, and stems or a mix someone else can use. Not "let's try again next week".

## Three steps

1. Before playing, fix tempo, key, section order and who counts in, and write it on one sheet.
2. Record whole takes during rehearsal, not fragments; one complete take with mistakes beats ten fragments.
3. Export the same day, name files song-instrument-date, keep stems aligned in start, rate and length, and write three lines on what to change.

## Pages and tools to use

- [Session prep checklist](https://prod.midicn.com/session-prep-checklist/) — decide everything possible early so people only have to play.
- [Multitrack recording](https://prod.midicn.com/multitrack-recording/) — isolation and headroom matter more than track count.
- [Collaboration and handoff](https://prod.midicn.com/collaboration-and-handoff/) — make the session explain itself before someone else opens it.
- [Stems delivery](https://prod.midicn.com/stems-delivery/) — agreed groups and names; never leave drums and bass in one file.
- [Stage and monitors](../culture/stage-and-monitors/) — when you cannot hear yourself, rehearsal efficiency drops fast.

## Acceptance checks

- Every song has one complete reference where verse, chorus and solo are audible.
- Stem names carry song, instrument and date; all share start point, sample rate and length.
- Files and the change list go out within 24 hours, and someone confirms receipt before the next rehearsal.
