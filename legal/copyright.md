## 第三方材料

音乐作品、演奏、录音、乐谱版本、音色文件及数据可能涉及不同权利。某首作品已进入公有领域，不代表所有录音、编曲或整理版本都可任意使用。

本站对第三方材料不提供统一授权。请到实例或曲目详情查看来源、许可标识和原始发布地址。C1 表示台账记载允许商业使用；C2 带非商业限制；C3 限于来源允许的学习、研究等用途。具体范围和条件以完整来源条款为准。

## 权利反馈

如果你认为某项内容的授权、署名或收录有问题，请到[项目反馈页](https://github.com/midicn/midi-library/issues)提供具体页面、作品标识及可核验依据。请勿在公开问题中提交不必要的个人资料。

## Site-owned content

Unless otherwise marked, the site's own text and diagrams use [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribute the source, link to the licence and indicate changes. Code licensing is stated in the relevant repository's MIT file.

## Third-party material

Compositions, performances, recordings, score editions, SoundFonts and datasets may involve different rights. A public-domain composition does not make every recording, arrangement or edition unrestricted.

The site grants no blanket licence over third-party material. Follow the example or track-detail links to provenance, licence markers and original releases. C1 denotes commercial use permitted in the ledger; C2 carries non-commercial restrictions; C3 is limited to the study, research or other uses allowed by the source. Full source terms define the scope and conditions.

## Rights enquiries

Use the [project issue page](https://github.com/midicn/midi-library/issues) to identify a disputed page or work and provide verifiable evidence about permission, attribution or inclusion. Avoid posting unnecessary personal information publicly.
