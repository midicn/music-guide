```

```

## 二、为什么可以给这么宽的授权

因为本站的内容**从源头就是干净的**：

- **文字 100% 原创** —— 参考源只用于核查事实，未采用任何来源的句式（尤其**未使用维基百科文本**，它的 CC BY-SA 具有传染性）
- **图表 100% 自绘 SVG** —— 未使用任何图库、教材插图、扫描谱
- **不使用实拍照片** —— 乐器与场景均为矢量示意
- **音频为浏览器实时合成** —— 不是任何录音制品的复制
- **未使用外部 CDN**（字体、图标、脚本均本地托管）

因此本站**有能力**给出 CC BY 4.0，而不是被迫保留所有权利。

| **C3** | 仅限学习研究 | 传统音乐整理（含中国民歌） |

**本站不改变这些曲目的原有许可**，也不对它们主张任何权利。
需要确认某首曲目的使用条件时，请以 **midicn-lib 的台账**为最终依据。

## 五、如果你的权利受到侵害

我们**愿意且会迅速**处理任何合理的权利主张。若你认为是本站内容的权利人，且认为本站使用方式超出了合理范围，请提供：

1. **具体的页面 URL**
2. **你主张权利的作品**（以及你在其中的权利性质）
3. **你的联系方式**
4. 一段说明：你为什么认为该使用未获授权

提交渠道：[GitHub 仓库](https://github.com/midicn) 提交 issue（若涉及隐私，可在 issue 中只留联系方式，我们私下沟通）。

**我们的处理承诺**：

- 收到有效通知后，**先下架、后核实**（不让争议内容继续在线）
- 若核实为误判，会说明原因并恢复
- 全程不要求你提供超出必要范围的信息

## 2. Why such a permissive licence is possible

Because the content is **clean at the source**:

- **Text is 100% original** — sources are used to verify facts, never for phrasing (**no Wikipedia text**, whose CC BY-SA is viral)
- **All diagrams are hand-drawn SVG** — no stock art, textbook figures or scanned scores
- **No photographic images** — instruments and scenes are vector illustrations
- **Audio is synthesised live in the browser** — not a copy of any recording
- **No external CDNs** (fonts, icons and scripts are all self-hosted)

That is why this site *can* grant CC BY 4.0 rather than reserving all rights.

## 3. Referenced tracks (MIDI)

Every playable example comes from the [midicn-lib](https://lib.midicn.com/) dataset with its licence tier **stated on the page**:

| Tier | Meaning | Typical sources |
|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Commercial use permitted | Public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, OPEN |
| **C2** | Non-commercial | CC BY-NC-SA (e.g. the aria classical piano set) |
| **C3** | Study and research only | Traditional music transcriptions (incl. Chinese folk songs) |

**This site does not alter those licences and claims no rights over those tracks.**
To confirm what you may do with a track, the **midicn-lib ledger** is the final authority.

## 5. If your rights are affected

We **will act promptly** on any reasonable claim. If you hold rights in material used here and believe our use exceeds what is permitted, please provide:

1. **The specific page URL**
2. **The work** you claim, and the nature of your rights in it
3. **How to reach you**
4. A short explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorised

Submit via an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/midicn) (if privacy matters, leave contact details only and we will follow up privately).

**Our commitments:**

- On a valid notice, we **take it down first and verify after** — disputed material does not stay online
- If the claim turns out to be mistaken, we explain why and restore it
- We will not ask for more information than necessary

## 6. What we do proactively

- At the dataset level, midicn-lib keeps **per-source licence records** and **removal logs** (including a documented review that removed 521 possibly in-copyright works from one source)
- At the content level, every entry lists its **sources** so facts can be traced
- If advertising or other commercialisation is introduced, we will **re-assess** the licence-tier risk of the tracks we reference
