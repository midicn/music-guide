## 使用前查看来源

音乐库、MIDI分类下载、下载包与音色库提供第三方材料的检索或下载入口。本站不主张这些作品的权利，也不为整个集合提供统一授权。

每次使用前，请核对具体曲目或文件的来源、许可版本和附加条件：

| 标识 | 查阅重点 |
|---|---|
| C1 | 台账记载允许商业使用，仍可能要求署名、许可链接或相同方式共享 |
| C2 | 带非商业限制，须核对完整条款 |
| C3 | 仅限来源允许的学习、研究等用途，不应推定可公开再分发或商用 |

分类不能代替完整许可。相同作品的不同编曲、录音、转录或音色文件，也可能有不同使用条件。

## 网站运营

页面可免费浏览，页脚广告支持网站运行。广告展示不改变任何文件的来源许可，也不授予额外的商业使用权。数据处理见[隐私政策](../../privacy/)。

下载和转载时请保留文件随附的来源与许可记录。需要批量材料时，优先使用提供的下载包，不以高频抓取影响他人访问。

## 疑问与更正

如果台账与原始来源不一致，应先核对来源；无法确认时，不要把不确定标注当作授权。可通过[项目反馈页](https://github.com/midicn/home/issues)提交文件标识、来源链接及依据。

## Check the source before use

The library, dimension browser, packs and SoundFont directory provide discovery or download access to third-party material. The site claims no rights over those works and grants no blanket licence for the collection.

Before using a track or file, check its source, licence version and conditions:

| Tier | What to check |
|---|---|
| C1 | The ledger records commercial permission; attribution, licence links or share-alike may still be required |
| C2 | Non-commercial restrictions apply; read the full terms |
| C3 | Limited to study, research or other source-permitted uses; do not assume public redistribution or commercial use is allowed |

A category does not replace a licence. Arrangements, recordings, transcriptions and SoundFont files associated with the same work can have different conditions.

## Website operation

Pages are free to browse. Footer advertising supports site operation but changes no file's source licence and grants no additional commercial rights. See the [privacy policy](../../privacy/) for data handling.

Retain provenance and licence records when downloading or redistributing. Use the supplied packs for bulk access rather than high-frequency scraping that disrupts other readers.

## Questions and corrections

If a ledger entry differs from the original release, check the source first. An uncertain label is not permission. Report a file identifier, source link and supporting evidence through the [project issue page](https://github.com/midicn/home/issues).
