## 数据可能有误

曲目与音色信息来自来源记录、转录及整理，可能存在标题不符、作者或年代错误、缺失字段、重复版本和不完整分类。未填写的字段表示没有足够信息，不应据此补成确定结论。

## 试听与文件

MIDI 描述演奏事件；浏览器试听受音色、音源实现、速度与设备影响，不能当作原演奏或录音的还原。不同文件也可能采用不同声部、力度和控制消息。

下载前后请核对文件标识及随附说明。某文件能播放，不代表它适合你的演出、教学、研究或商业交付。

## 许可边界

逐曲标注尽量保留来源，但可能继承上游错误。完整原始许可优先于简化标签；有疑问时应核查来源或向权利方确认，不能把本站分类当作保证。

## 反馈

请在[项目反馈页](https://github.com/midicn/midi-library/issues)提供具体文件 ID、问题位置及可复现步骤。涉及授权时，同时附原始发布地址和相关条款。

## Data can contain errors

Music and SoundFont information is compiled from source records and transcriptions. Titles, authors, dates or classifications may be wrong, incomplete or duplicated. An empty field means insufficient information, not an invitation to fill in an unsupported answer.

## Playback and files

MIDI describes performance events. Browser playback depends on the SoundFont, engine, speed and device; it is not a reconstruction of the original performance or recording. Files may also differ in parts, dynamics and controller messages.

Check file identifiers and accompanying notes. A playable file is not necessarily suitable for a particular performance, lesson, study or commercial delivery.

## Licence limitations

Per-track records aim to retain provenance but may inherit upstream errors. Full original terms take precedence over simplified labels. Verify uncertain permissions with the source or rights holder rather than treating a site category as a guarantee.

## Feedback

Provide the file ID, issue location and reproducible steps on the [project issue page](https://github.com/midicn/midi-library/issues). For licensing questions, include the original release address and relevant terms.
