## 一、数据的性质

midicn-lib 的数据**来自上游来源的转录与整理**，不是我们重新录入或校订的。

这意味着数据质量**取决于上游**：上游准确，我们才准确；上游有误，我们可能继承该错误。

## 二、我们不保证什么

- ❌ **不保证元数据完全准确** —— 作曲家、年代、作品号、调性等字段可能有误
- ❌ **不保证文件与标题完全对应** —— 少数文件的实际内容可能与标题不符
- ❌ **不保证覆盖完整** —— 某些作曲家的作品在库中可能只有一部分
- ❌ **不保证许可标注绝对无误** —— 见下条

## 三、许可标注的边界（重要）

我们尽力做到**逐曲标注来源与许可**，但：

> **本站在转录过程中可能出现标注误差。判断某首曲目的使用条件时，请以其来源的原始许可为最终依据。**

我们的做法是"**留空不猜**"：不确定的字段宁可留空，不写推测值。但许可字段的标注仍可能受上游元数据质量影响。

若要确认某一首的使用条件，建议：
1. 查看曲目页面标注的**来源**
2. 到该来源的原始发布处核对许可条款
3. 有疑问时联系我们

## 四、元数据的"留空不猜"原则

你可能会注意到库中有**大量空字段**（如某首曲目没有年代）。

这是**有意为之**：

- 我们**不填推测值**（不写"约 1700 年"这类猜测）
- 我们**不使用模糊占位**（不写"欧洲""Unknown"充数）
- 空着，意味着"上游没有这个信息，我们也不知道"

**空字段比错误字段更有用** —— 它至少不会误导你。

## 五、MIDI 文件本身

- MIDI 是**演奏信息的描述**，不是录音 —— 同一首曲子的不同 MIDI 文件听起来可能差别很大，取决于制作者的演奏处理
- 库中文件的**演奏质量参差不齐**（有专业制谱，也有爱好者转录）
- 文件可播放性经过检查，但**不保证在所有设备/软件上都正常**

## 六、与知识站的关系

[midicn.com 及各知识站](https://midicn.com/) 引用本数据集的曲目作为**教学示例**。那边的内容是我们原创的，规则与本站不同 —— 请分别理解。

## 七、不构成专业意见

本站提供的是**数据与检索服务**，不构成：

- **法律意见**（许可、授权、合同请咨询律师）
- **音乐学或考据结论**（数据仅供参考，研究请回溯原始来源）
- **任何形式的担保**

## 1. What this data is

midicn-lib's data is **transcribed and organised from upstream sources**; we did not re-enter or re-edit it.

That means quality **depends on upstream**: accurate there, accurate here — and errors there may be inherited here.

## 2. What we do not warrant

- ❌ **Metadata is not guaranteed accurate** — composer, era, catalogue number, key and similar fields may be wrong
- ❌ **A file may not match its title** — in a few cases the musical content differs from the label
- ❌ **Coverage is not guaranteed complete** — a given composer's output may only be partly present
- ❌ **Licence labels are not guaranteed error-free** — see below

## 3. The limits of licence labelling (important)

We try to state the **source and licence per track**, but:

> **Transcription errors are possible on our side. To determine what you may do with a work, its source's original licence is the final authority.**

Our rule is "**leave blank rather than guess**": uncertain fields stay empty rather than carrying a conjecture. Licence fields, however, still depend on upstream metadata quality.

To confirm a specific work:
1. Check the **source** shown on its page
2. Verify the licence at that source's original publication
3. Contact us if in doubt

## 4. Why so many fields are blank

You may notice **many empty fields** (a work with no date, for instance).

This is **deliberate**:

- We **do not fill in guesses** (no "circa 1700")
- We **do not use vague placeholders** (no "Europe", no "Unknown" as filler)
- Blank means: "upstream does not have this, and neither do we"

**A blank field is more useful than a wrong one** — at least it does not mislead you.

## 5. About the MIDI files

- MIDI describes **performance instructions**, not a recording — the same piece can sound very different depending on who made the file
- **Quality varies** across the library (professional engraving alongside hobbyist transcriptions)
- Playability is checked, but **not guaranteed on every device or player**

## 6. Relation to the knowledge sites

[midicn.com and the knowledge sites](https://midicn.com/) reference tracks from this dataset as **teaching examples**. Their content is written by us and follows different rules — read each accordingly.

## 7. Not professional advice

This site provides **data and retrieval**, not:

- **Legal advice** (for licensing, contracts, consult a lawyer)
- **Musicological findings** (the data is a reference; research should return to the original sources)
- **Any form of warranty**

Content concerning China's geography, ethnic groups and music follows the **official position of the People's Republic of China** (e.g. we write "Taiwan, China", "Hong Kong, China", "Macao, China").
