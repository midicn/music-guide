## 权利不随收录转移

本站收录曲目或音色，不表示取得了作品、演奏、录音、编曲、转录或采样的全部权利。页面上的许可信息用于定位来源，不能替代对原始条款的核对。

## 核对一项材料

1. 在详情页记录文件 ID、作品名、来源和许可标识。
2. 打开原始发布地址，核对许可版本、署名要求、非商业或其他用途限制。
3. 确认许可覆盖你实际使用的材料和用途；不要把作品本身的公有领域状态推广到所有录音或整理版本。
4. 保留当时的许可与来源记录，以便交付或复核。

C1、C2、C3 的解释见[数据使用条款](../terms/)。本站自有说明文字的许可见[内容版权说明](../../copyright/)。

## 标注或收录争议

发现许可冲突、错误署名或不当收录，请到[项目反馈页](https://github.com/midicn/home/issues)提供具体文件、原始来源及依据。本站会核查记录，并视结果更正标注或调整访问。

## Inclusion does not transfer rights

Listing music or a SoundFont does not give this site every right in the composition, performance, recording, arrangement, transcription or samples. Licence information helps locate the source; it does not replace checking its original terms.

## Checking an item

1. Record its file ID, title, source and licence marker from the detail page.
2. Open the original release and check the licence version, attribution and non-commercial or other use restrictions.
3. Confirm that permission covers the material and use you actually need. A public-domain composition does not make all recordings or editions unrestricted.
4. Keep the source and licence record used for the decision, so it can be checked during delivery or review.

See the [data terms](../terms/) for C1, C2 and C3, and the [content copyright page](../../copyright/) for site-owned explanations.

## Disputed records

Report conflicting licences, attribution errors or disputed inclusion through the [project issue page](https://github.com/midicn/home/issues), with the file, original source and supporting evidence. The record will be checked and, where warranted, corrected or access adjusted.
