::: zh
「脑子里有一句旋律，或者一段词，但一直没做成一首能听完的歌。」这条路从一句动机走到能发出去的 Demo，中间只做必要的决定。

## 这条路径适合谁

适合已经能哼出完整段落、愿意改十遍的人。会一点乐器会更顺，不会也能在软件里点出来。不适合只想贴画面的人——那是[给视频或游戏配乐](../path-score/)。

## 先读这三页

- [动机与主题](https://theo.midicn.com/motive/) —— 动机是材料，主题是陈述；先分清这两件事，你才知道手上那半句是什么。
- [歌曲形式](https://theo.midicn.com/song-form/) —— 主歌与副歌的反复回归决定听众记不记得，结构立住再谈音色。
- [三个最常见的和声进行](../tools/progression-basic/) —— 一副能承载旋律的骨架，比一堆和弦名有用。

## 接下来

- [旋律的构成](https://theo.midicn.com/melody/) —— 音阶是素材，旋律是从中做出的选择。
- [终止式](https://theo.midicn.com/cadence/) —— 和声的标点，决定句子在哪收。
- [记忆点设计](https://prod.midicn.com/hook-design/) —— 短、可重复、可变形，出现三次才站得住。
- [副歌的推力设计](https://prod.midicn.com/chorus-lift/) —— 推力来自换挡，不来自音量。
- [数字音频工作站](https://prod.midicn.com/daw-introduction/) —— 把想法变成文件的地方。
- [从灵感到成品](https://prod.midicn.com/idea-to-finished-track/) —— 给中间过程设几道关口。

## 可以跳过

混音与母带的具体手法、配器与管弦乐、AI 提示词、完整的乐理体系。歌还没立住时，这些都是在优化错的东西。

## 常见走弯路

1. 先学和声学再动笔。写歌靠判断，判断靠写完几首。
2. 每首写到副歌就换新的。十首半成品不如一首完整的。
3. 用音色和混音掩盖结构问题。结构不对，越混越假。

::: en
There is a melody line, or a verse, in your head, and it has never become a song anyone can sit through. This path runs from one motive to a demo you can send, making only the decisions that matter.

## Who this path is for

For anyone who can already hum full sections and is willing to revise ten times. A little instrument helps; none is required if you can click notes in. Not for scoring to picture — that is [Scoring for Video or Games](../path-score/).

## Read these three first

- [Motive and Subject](https://theo.midicn.com/motive/) — a motive is material, a subject is a statement; tell them apart and you will know what that half-line in your head is.
- [Song Form](https://theo.midicn.com/song-form/) — the chorus returning is what makes a song memorable; fix the structure before the timbre.
- [The three most common progressions](../tools/progression-basic/) — a skeleton that can carry a melody beats a pile of chord names.

## What comes next

- [The Making of a Melody](https://theo.midicn.com/melody/) — a scale is material; a melody is a selection from it.
- [Cadence](https://theo.midicn.com/cadence/) — the punctuation of harmony; it decides where a phrase closes.
- [Hook design](https://prod.midicn.com/hook-design/) — short, repeatable, transformable; it takes three appearances to stick.
- [Designing the chorus lift](https://prod.midicn.com/chorus-lift/) — lift comes from changing gear, not from volume.
- [The DAW](https://prod.midicn.com/daw-introduction/) — where an idea becomes a file.
- [From idea to finished track](https://prod.midicn.com/idea-to-finished-track/) — put checkpoints in the middle.

## What you can skip

The specifics of mixing and mastering, orchestration, AI prompting, the full theory curriculum. Before the song stands up, all of it is optimising the wrong thing.

## Common detours

1. Studying harmony before writing. Songwriting runs on judgement, and judgement comes from finishing songs.
2. Abandoning each song at the chorus. Ten half-songs are worth less than one complete one.
3. Using timbre and mixing to hide structural problems. The structure is wrong; the more you mix, the faker it gets.
