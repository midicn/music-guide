::: zh
「想学，但不知道学什么、从哪一步开始。」这条路径管的是从选琴到能完整弹一首的全过程：手上的重复，加上一点判断。

## 这条路径适合谁

适合愿意每天重复二十分钟、接受进步以周计的人；也适合家里有件乐器落灰、现在想认真开始的人。不适合只想理解音乐的人——先走[听懂音乐](../path-listen/)。

## 先读这三页

- [从零开始学音乐，第一步做什么](../start/first-steps/) —— 先定方向再花钱，这一页决定你后面三个月的钱花在哪。
- [钢琴、电钢还是键盘](../start/piano-vs-keyboard/) —— 最常见的第一个分岔：三件东西对应三种需求，先问需求再问型号。
- [钢琴](https://inst.midicn.com/piano/) —— 看清一件乐器的音域、发声方式与限制，你才知道「练它」意味着什么。

## 接下来

- [每天该练多久](../start/daily-practice-time/) —— 频率比时长重要，先把节奏定下来。
- [怎么练才有效](../start/deliberate-practice/) —— 把「练」拆成定义问题、缩小范围、立刻反馈。
- [不识谱，能学音乐吗](../start/cant-read-notation/) —— 能开始，但识谱是值得补的能力。
- [练习方法](https://theo.midicn.com/practice-method/) —— 练难的地方，不练整首。
- [买什么乐器：按预算分级](../start/budget-instrument/) —— 预算不是买不买得起，是怎么分配。

## 可以跳过

混音与母带、配器、AI 生成、和声学。你现在需要的是每周稳定的重复和一首能弹完的曲子，不是更多知识入口。

## 常见走弯路

1. 先买贵琴，再发现自己不喜欢。先用[按预算分级](../start/budget-instrument/)和[二手乐器](../start/used-instruments/)试三个月。
2. 每周换一首新曲。手还没记住，注意力先散了。
3. 只练曲子不练音阶，或只练音阶不碰曲子。两边都要留一点。
:::

You want to learn, but you have not chosen an instrument and do not know step one. This path covers the whole run from choosing an instrument to playing a piece end to end: repetition in the hands, plus a little judgement.

## Who this path is for

For anyone willing to repeat twenty minutes a day and accept progress measured in weeks; also for anyone with an instrument gathering dust who now wants to start properly. Not for pure listeners — take [Understanding What You Hear](../path-listen/) instead.

## Read these three first

- [First steps from zero](../start/first-steps/) — set a direction before spending money; this page decides where the next three months of budget go.
- [Piano, digital piano or keyboard](../start/piano-vs-keyboard/) — the most common first fork: three objects for three needs, so ask about the need before the model.
- [Piano](https://inst.midicn.com/piano/) — see one instrument's range, sound production and limits, and you will know what "practising it" actually asks of you.

## What comes next

- [How long should I practise each day?](../start/daily-practice-time/) — frequency beats duration; fix the rhythm first.
- [How to practise so it works](../start/deliberate-practice/) — define the problem, narrow the range, get feedback at once.
- [Can I learn music without reading notation?](../start/cant-read-notation/) — yes, you can start, but notation is worth adding.
- [Practice method](https://theo.midicn.com/practice-method/) — practise the hard bars, not the whole piece.
- [What instrument to buy: by budget](../start/budget-instrument/) — budget is not can-I-afford-it, it is how to allocate.

## What you can skip

Mixing and mastering, orchestration, AI generation, harmony. What you need is steady weekly repetition and one piece you can finish, not more entry points.

## Common detours

1. Buying an expensive instrument before knowing you like it. Try three months with [budget tiers](../start/budget-instrument/) and [used instruments](../start/used-instruments/).
2. A new piece every week. Your hands never memorise anything before your attention moves on.
3. Only pieces and no scales, or only scales and no pieces. Keep a little of both.
:::
