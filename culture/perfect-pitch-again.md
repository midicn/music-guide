::: zh
绝对音感大概是音乐心理学里**最被神化**的一项能力：仿佛有了它，音乐之门才对你敞开。把它放回研究的视野里，真实的图景要朴素得多——也友好得多。

## 它到底是什么

**绝对音感 = 听到一个音（或一组音），能直接说出它们的音名**（"这是升 F"），不需要参照音。与它相对的是**相对音感**：给你一个参照，你能判断其他音之间的关系——**后者才是音乐实践里真正日用的能力**。

## 谁有它、它从哪来

- **比例不高**：在普通人群与职业音乐家中都属少数（不同研究给的比例差异很大，与取样方法有关——本文不引具体数字）；
- **关键期与语言**：与**早期（幼年）音乐环境**关系密切；有研究发现**声调语言**使用者的比例相对较高——提示"把音高当作语言特征来加工"的环境可能有促进作用；
- **成年之后**：完全"从零长出来"的案例罕见，但**训练能明显提升**音高的辨识与记忆——只是通常到不了"脱口而出"的名。
  
  （围绕"先天还是习得占多少"的研究仍在继续；本节如实呈现观察，不下定论。）

## 它的边界：三个常被忽略的事实

1. **拥有者也会"偏"**：整套音高感可能整体偏移（如整体偏低半音），且不少拥有者会随年龄漂移；
2. **它不是职业门槛**：大量职业音乐家没有绝对音感——他们靠相对音感工作，没有任何损失；
3. **它不保证音乐性**：给音命名的能力与"把音乐弹好听"是两码事。

## 对普通学习者的真正启发

- **不要再等"天赋降临"**：你需要的是**相对音感**——它可训练、见效快（方法见 [[concept:ear-training|练耳]] 一节）；
- **命名是结果，不是起点**：先听"关系"（这是几度、走向哪里），命名会作为副产品慢慢到来；
- **校准用工具**：[[concept:concert-pitch|标准音高]] 与调音器，让练习有客观参照。

## 听一听：没有参照音，你能命名吗？

两个音。如果你没有绝对音感，多半说不出它们的名字——**但这不影响你判断它们"隔了多远"**。后者就是可训练的相对音感。

Perfect pitch is arguably the **most mythologised** ability in music psychology — as if the door to music only opens with it. Put it back under the research lens and the picture is plainer — and friendlier.

## What it actually is

**Perfect pitch = hearing a note (or chord) and naming it** ("that is F-sharp") without a reference. Its counterpart is **relative pitch**: given a reference, you judge how other notes relate — **the latter is the daily working skill of actual music-making**.

## Who has it, where it comes from

- **Uncommon**: a minority among both the general public and professional musicians (reported proportions vary widely with sampling — no specific figures here);
- **Critical period and language**: closely tied to **early childhood musical environment**; some studies find higher rates among **tonal-language** speakers — suggesting an environment that treats pitch as a language feature may help;
- **After childhood**: cases of it "growing from zero" in adults are rare, but **training clearly improves** pitch identification and memory — usually short of effortless naming.

  (Research on the innate/learned mix continues; this section reports observations without verdicts.)

## Its edges: three often-missed facts

1. **Even possessors drift**: the whole internal pitch grid may sit off (a semitone low, say), and many see it shift with age;
2. **It is not a professional gate**: many working musicians lack it — they run on relative pitch with no loss;
3. **It does not guarantee musicianship**: naming notes and making music beautiful are different abilities.

## What it really means for learners

- **Stop waiting for the gift**: what you need is **relative pitch** — trainable, fast to improve (see [[concept:ear-training|ear training]]);
- **Naming is a by-product, not a starting point**: hear *relations* first (which interval, which direction); names arrive later on their own;
- **Calibrate with tools**: [[concept:concert-pitch|concert pitch]] and a tuner give your practice an objective reference.

## Listen: no reference — can you name them?

Two notes. Without perfect pitch you likely cannot name them — **but that does not stop you hearing "how far apart"**. That second skill is the trainable one.
