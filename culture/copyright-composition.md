「这首歌是谁的？」——这个问题之所以难回答，是因为**一首歌在制度上不是一件东西，而是一叠东西**。把这一叠数清楚，后面的问题就都好办了。

（本文只讲结构与机制，不构成法律意见；具体情况请咨询专业人士。）

## 第一层：词曲作品

**词、曲是两个可以分开的创作**，各自有作者（有时是同一人，有时是两批人）。这一层的正式说法是**「音乐作品」**（含词与曲）。

- 它的成立**不需要登记**——创作完成即产生（登记只是方便举证）；
- 它保护的是**旋律、和声、歌词等创作要素**，而不是「这首歌的声音」；
- 作者可以把它**转让或许可**给他人（出版社、平台、任何机构）——但**转让的通常是财产权，署名权等人身性的权利另说**（见[著作人身权与财产权](../moral-vs-economic/)）。

## 第二层：录音

把作品「录下来」这件事，会产生**一个新的权利客体**：录音制品（唱片、音频文件）。它通常属于**录音制作者**——出钱、组织这次录制的人或公司。

**关键点：录音的权利和词曲的权利互不相干。** 同一首歌可以有无数个录音版本，每个版本各归各的录制者；而词曲那一层，从头到尾只是一份。

## 附带的一层：表演

演绎这首作品的**表演者**（歌手、乐手）对自己这一次表演，也有受保护的权利——它属于「邻接权」家族（见[邻接权](../neighboring-rights/)）。

## 为什么必须分清

因为**使用一首歌的动作，落在哪一层，决定了要找谁**：

- 你要**重新录唱**一首歌 → 处理词曲层（机械许可）；
- 你要把**某个录音**放进视频 → 处理录音层（+词曲层）；
- 你要**公开播放** → 落在表演/广播相关的通道。

「一首歌有两层主人」听起来麻烦，但它是产业清晰运转的前提——每一层都能独立谈判、独立收费、独立授权。

## 中国语境的用词

中国著作权法把录音这一层称为**「录音制品」**，与「音乐作品」并列；制度结构与国际通行体系大体对应，具体规则与救济方式有自己的特点（详见[中国著作权法的音乐条款](../china-copyright-notes/)）。

"Whose song is this?" is a hard question because **a song is not one thing in law but a stack of things**. Count the stack once, and everything downstream gets easier.

(This article explains structure, not legal advice; consult a professional for specific cases.)

## Layer one: the musical work

**Words and music are separable creations**, each with authors (sometimes one person, sometimes two teams). The formal name for this layer is the **musical work** (lyrics and music).

- It exists **without registration** — it is born when creation is finished (registration only helps prove it);
- It protects the **created elements**: melody, harmony, lyrics — not "the sound of this song";
- Authors may **assign or license** it (to publishers, platforms, anyone) — but what moves is usually the economic side; moral rights like attribution are treated separately (see [moral rights and economic rights](../moral-vs-economic/)).

## Layer two: the recording

Recording a work creates **a new protected object**: the sound recording (a disc, an audio file). It usually belongs to the **producer of the recording** — whoever financed and organised that session.

**Crucially: the recording's rights and the work's rights are independent.** One song may have countless recordings, each owned by its own producer — while the work layer remains a single thing throughout.

## An attached layer: performance

The **performers** (singer, players) in a given performance hold rights in that performance — part of the "neighbouring rights" family (see [neighbouring rights](../neighboring-rights/)).

## Why the layers must be kept apart

Because **which layer your action lands on decides whom you must talk to**:

- **Re-recording** a song → the work layer (mechanical licence);
- Putting **a particular recording** into a video → the recording layer (plus the work layer);
- **Public playback** → the performance/broadcast channels.

"Two owners for one song" sounds like trouble; it is actually the premise of a functioning industry — each layer negotiates, charges and licenses independently.

## Wording in the Chinese context

Chinese copyright law names the recording layer **"sound recording product"**, parallel to the "musical work"; the structure broadly matches the international system, with its own rules and remedies (see [Chinese copyright notes](../china-copyright-notes/)).
