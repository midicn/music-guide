::: zh
一首歌在脑子里循环播放，停不下来——英语里管它叫 **earworm（耳虫）**，这个现象几乎人人都经历过。它其实是一个**正常系统的正常故障**。

## 什么样的旋律容易「上脑」

心理学研究对这个现象的观察比较一致，易感旋律通常具备：

- **简单的结构**：短句 + 重复；
- **规整的起伏**：音高走向熟悉、跨越不大（可以哼）；
- **轻快的节奏**：比慢歌更容易"带起来"；
- **一点点特别之处**：某个转折或跳进让它"脱颖而出"。

（顺带一个反直觉的观察：**不是只有讨厌的歌会变耳虫**——喜欢的歌同样，甚至更多。）

## 为什么赶不走

主流解释是：**耳虫是记忆系统正常运转的副产品**——音乐被加工成"可复述的短单元"（大约几秒到十几秒），这个单元会自动重播，就像后台进程。**你不能"删掉"它，只能让它自然结束或转移注意力。**

## 怎么应对：按证据强度分级

**相对有支持的做法**：

- **把它听完**（特别是觉得"卡在中间"时——给片段一个"结尾"）；
- **转移"听觉注意力"**：听另一首完整的、结构不同的歌；
- **占用"语音回路"**：朗读、跟人说话、大声念一段文字。

**民间常用、证据较弱**：嚼口香糖、默念别的旋律（有时反而"以毒攻毒"失败）。

**一个不值得做的**：跟它较劲、试图强行"静音"——越压制越活跃（这是注意力的老规律）。

## 什么时候要留意

偶尔的耳虫是常态；但如果它**持续数天、影响睡眠、反复到痛苦程度**，且伴随焦虑或强迫症状——那属于需要专业评估的范畴（极少数人报告过"耳虫失控"的困扰）。

## 顺带：把耳虫变成工具

- **学曲目时**：用"简单短句"的天然粘性——把难记的段落改写成带钩子的小单元；
- **写歌时**：钩子工学的第一步，就是做出"可循环、可哼、有转折"的短句（见 [[concept:melody|旋律]]）。

One song, looping in your head, refusing to stop — English calls it an **earworm**, and almost everyone has hosted one. It is, in fact, **a normal glitch of a normal system**.

## Which melodies get stuck

Research sketches a fairly consistent profile:

- **Simple structure**: short phrase + repetition;
- **Even contour**: familiar shapes, few large leaps (hummable);
- **Lively tempo**: catchier than slow songs;
- **A small oddity**: one turn or leap that makes it stand out.

(A counter-intuitive note: **it is not only annoying songs** — liked songs become earworms just as often, or more.)

## Why it will not leave

The mainstream account: **an earworm is a by-product of a working memory system** — music is chunked into hummable units (seconds to a dozen or so), and the unit auto-replays like a background process. **You cannot delete it; you can only let it end or redirect attention.**

## Coping: graded by evidence

**Relatively supported**:

- **Listen through to the end** (especially when stuck mid-phrase — give the fragment a closing);
- **Redirect auditory attention**: play a different, complete song with another shape;
- **Occupy the "phonological loop"**: read aloud, talk to someone, say a paragraph out loud.

**Folk remedies, weaker evidence**: chewing gum, silently reciting another tune (which sometimes fails spectacularly).

**What not to do**: fighting it, trying to force a "mute" — suppression makes it louder (the old law of attention).

## When to take notice

Occasional earworms are ordinary. But if one **lasts days, disturbs sleep, or recurs painfully**, alongside anxiety or obsessive symptoms, it belongs to professional assessment (a small minority report truly distressing "stuck song" states).

## Reversing it into a tool

- **When learning**: exploit the stickiness of short repeated units — rewrite hard passages into hummable chunks;
- **When writing**: hook engineering begins with "loopable, hummable, one small turn" (see [[concept:melody|melody]]).
