::: zh
「我想翻唱这首歌」——先做一个选择题：**你是要重新唱一遍（翻唱），还是直接搬原曲（复制）？** 两条轨道要过的关完全不同。

（制度梳理，不构成法律意见。）

## 轨道一：翻唱（重新演唱、重新录制）

你用的是**词曲**这一层。要过的关是：

- **获得词曲层的许可**——通常通过集体管理组织或词曲版权方；
- **支付相应的报酬**——按约定的方式结算；
- 并履行**署名**等义务。

**一个常被忽略的特别安排**：对于**已被合法录制为录音制品的音乐作品**，中国著作权法给了"重新录制"一个特别通道（法定许可的情形之一，**附带条件**：著作权人声明不许使用的除外，且须按规定付酬）。具体适用请核对现行条款——**别把"有特别通道"理解成"完全不用管"**。

## 轨道二：复制（直接用原录音）

你想**直接用原版录音**（放进视频、当背景、做混剪）——这要过的是**录音层**的关（何况还有词曲层）：

- 要向**录音制作者**（唱片公司等）取得许可；
- 常见的形式是**同步许可 + 录音使用许可**两份（见[音乐授权家族](../licensing-family/)）。

**两条轨道的分界，就是常识的分界**：你嗓子唱的是词曲；你播放的是录音。

## 几个实战问题

- **"我在短视频平台发翻唱"**：平台与集体管理组织之间常有**一揽子安排**——按平台流程走、让平台完成结算，通常是最规范的路径；
- **"我用原曲伴奏翻唱"**：伴奏=原录音 → 同时踩到录音层，比纯翻唱多一道关；
- **"我改了词/改了曲"**：那就进入了**改编**地带，要另行处理改编相关权利（见[著作人身权与财产权](../moral-vs-economic/)）；
- **"我只是发到网上不赚钱"**：非营利**不是**自动的通行证（见[合理使用](../fair-use/)）。

## 一份最小行动清单

1. 明确要用**哪一层**（词曲？录音？还是都要）；
2. 找到对应权利的**集体管理组织/平台流程/版权方**；
3. 保留**授权与结算的凭证**；
4. 不确定就选"不需要争议"的方案（开放许可作品、公版作品）。

## 听一听：两把「同一首歌」

同一串音，演唱版本与演奏版本气质不同——就像翻唱与原版的关系：**同一层词曲，两件不同的制品。**

::: en
"I want to cover this song" — choose first: **re-sing it (a cover) or take the original recording (a copy)?** Two tracks, two different sets of gates.

(Rules, not legal advice.)

## Track one: the cover (re-recording)

You are using the **work layer**. The gates:

- **Obtain a licence for words and music** — usually via a collective management organisation or the publisher;
- **Pay the due royalties** — by the agreed settlement;
- Meet duties such as **attribution**.

**A special arrangement easily missed**: for musical works **already lawfully recorded as sound recordings**, Chinese law gives re-recording a particular channel (one of the statutory-licence situations — with conditions: the rightsholder may declare prohibition, and payment is required). Check the current text for real cases — **"a special channel exists" never means "nothing to handle".**

## Track two: the copy (using the original)

Using the **original recording** (in a video, as a bed, in a mashup) calls at the **recording layer** (plus the work layer):

- Clear with the **producer** (label, etc.);
- Typically as **two licences: sync + master** (see [the licensing family](../licensing-family/)).

**The dividing line is common sense**: what your voice sings is the work; what you play back is the recording.

## Field questions

- **"I post covers on short-video platforms"**: platforms and collectives often have **umbrella arrangements** — following the platform flow and letting it settle is the compliant path;
- **"I use the original backing track"**: that backing *is* the original recording — you have stepped onto the recording layer, one gate more than a pure cover;
- **"I changed the words/music"**: now you are in **adaptation** territory, a separate set of rights (see [moral and economic rights](../moral-vs-economic/));
- **"It is non-commercial"**: non-profit is **not** an automatic pass (see [fair use](../fair-use/)).

## A minimal action list

1. Decide the **layer** (work? recording? both?);
2. Find the **collective / platform flow / rightsholder** for it;
3. Keep **proof of licence and settlement**;
4. When unsure, choose the dispute-free path (openly licensed or public-domain material).

## Listen: two fiddles on one tune

The same notes, sung or played, differ in character — exactly like a cover versus an original: **one work layer, two different products.**
