版权话题的误区有一个共同特征：**每一句都「听起来对」。** 逐条清点——这次连回应带去处，一次到位。

（通识汇总，不构成法律意见。）

## 十条说法，十条回应

1. **「没标 © 就没有版权。」** → 错。著作权**创作完成即产生**，不需要加标记（见[一首歌上有几层权利](../copyright-composition/)）。
2. **「登记了才有版权。」** → 错。登记只影响**举证便利**，不影响权利存在。
3. **「改到 30% 以上就没事。」** → 错。没有"百分比安全线"这回事——判定看实质性相似等综合要素（见[采样与二次创作](../sampling-and-transform/)）。
4. **「非营利就随便用。」** → 错。非营利不等于合理使用（见[合理使用](../fair-use/)）。
5. **「标了来源就可以用。」** → 错。署名是义务，**不是许可证**。
6. **「公版作品随便用。」** → 半对。公版的是**作品**；你手上那份**录音/版本**可能是另一回事（见[公有领域判定](../public-domain/)）。
7. **「翻唱随便发。」** → 大概率错。翻唱要走词曲层通道；用原伴奏还要过录音层（见[翻唱要不要授权](../cover-vs-copy/)）。
8. **「买了 CD / 充了会员 = 可以放进我的视频。」** → 错。购买的是**收听的权利**，不是配乐的权利（见[给视频配乐](../sync-license/)）。
9. **「没人管的歌就是无主的。」** → 错。找不到权利人是"先别用"的信号，不是"自由"的信号；也有专门机制处理真正的无主作品，但不适用于"我没去查"。
10. **「用了很久没人找我 = 合法了。」** → 错。维权有时效，但"没被发现"从来不是权利状态。

## 把误区串起来看

十条里藏着同一个形状：**把「感觉合理」当成了「制度合法」。** 制度的答案从来只有三个来源——**法条、合同、条款**——而不是直觉。

## 一份正确姿势清单

- 使用前：查**层级**（作品/录音）→ 查**期限** → 查**使用地** → 查**版本**；
- 使用时：**能开放许可就不清版权，能公版就不开许可**；
- 使用后：**保留全套凭证**。

Copyright myths share one trait: **every one "sounds right".** Taken one by one — reply and destination in a single pass.

(Summary, not legal advice.)

## Ten claims, ten replies

1. **"No ©, no copyright."** Wrong. Copyright exists **upon creation** — no mark required (see [how many rights live in one song](../copyright-composition/)).
2. **"Registration creates copyright."** Wrong. Registration only helps with **proof**, not existence.
3. **"Change 30% and it is fine."** Wrong. There is no percentage safe line — judgement rests on substantial similarity and other factors (see [sampling and transformation](../sampling-and-transform/)).
4. **"Non-profit means anything goes."** Wrong. Non-commercial is not fair use (see [fair use](../fair-use/)).
5. **"Credit the source and use it."** Wrong. Attribution is a duty, **not a licence**.
6. **"Public domain means use freely."** Half right. The **work** is free; the **recording or edition** in your hands may be another matter (see [public-domain reckoning](../public-domain/)).
7. **"Just post the cover."** Mostly wrong. Covers walk the work-layer channel; the original backing also touches the recording layer (see [covering vs copying](../cover-vs-copy/)).
8. **"Bought the CD / subscribed = may soundtrack my video."** Wrong. You bought the **right to listen**, not the right to sync (see [music for video](../sync-license/)).
9. **"Nobody manages it, so it's free."** Wrong. Not finding the owner is a signal to *wait*, not a licence — dedicated orphan-works mechanisms exist, but not for "I didn't look".
10. **"Used it for years, nobody complained."** Wrong. Limitation periods exist; being unnoticed is never a rights status.

## The shape behind the ten

One pattern: **mistaking "feels reasonable" for "is legal".** The system's answers take only three sources — **statute, contract, terms** — never intuition.

## A checklist of the right posture

- Before: check the **layer** (work/recording) → **term** → **place of use** → **version**;
- During: **open licence first, then public domain, then clearance**;
- After: **keep the whole paper trail**.

## Listen: two layers speaking separately
