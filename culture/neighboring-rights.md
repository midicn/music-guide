::: zh
如果说著作权保护的是**写出曲子的人**，那么邻接权保护的是**把曲子弹出来、录下来的人**——他们不"创作"旋律，但他们的工作同样值得被制度承认。

（制度介绍，不构成法律意见。）

## 邻接权的三家住户

中国著作权法里说的**「与著作权有关的权利」**（通行叫法：邻接权），主要有三位住户：

1. **表演者**（演员、歌手、乐手）：对自己的表演有权利；
2. **录音录像制作者**：对制作出来的录音录像制品有权利；
3. **广播电台、电视台**：对播出的节目信号有权利。

**为什么叫"邻接"**：它们与著作权"相邻"——由同一批作品派生，但不是同一批权利。

## 表演者权：不只是"唱的人"

表演者的权利同样一分为二：

- **人身性的**：表明表演者身份、保护表演形象不受歪曲；
- **财产性的**：许可他人现场直播、录音录像、复制发行、信息网络传播，并由此获酬。

一个常见误区：**「词曲作者同意了，就能随便用这个演唱版本」——不一定。** 词曲层与表演层的许可是两回事（加上录音层就是三层，见[一首歌上有几层权利](../copyright-composition/)）。

## 录音制作者权：唱片公司那一层

录音制作者（出钱组织录制的人或机构）对自己制作的录音制品享有复制、发行、出租、信息网络传播等权利——**你在平台上听到的每一个音频文件背后，站着的就是这一层。**

## 为什么普通人也要懂一点

- **翻唱发行**：除了词曲层，若使用原伴奏（=原录音）还要录音层许可；
- **转载现场视频**：可能同时碰表演者与录制者；
- **二次创作**：剪视频、混剪、做混音——每一层都要过（详见[采样与二次创作](../sampling-and-transform/)）。

**一句话记忆：词曲、表演、录音，三层各归各主，使用它，就逐层打招呼。**

## 听一听：同一条素材，不同的「声音处理」

同一条旋律，换一个人演奏、换一种音色处理，就是另一份制品——这就是邻接权要保护的"每一层的工作"。

::: en
If copyright protects **those who write the music**, neighbouring rights protect **those who play it and record it** — not composers of melody, but work the system equally recognises.

(Rules, not legal advice.)

## Three households under the roof

In Chinese law, rights "related to copyright" have three main residents:

1. **Performers** (actors, singers, players): rights in their own performances;
2. **Producers of sound/film recordings**: rights in the recordings they make;
3. **Broadcasters**: rights in the signals they broadcast.

**Why "neighbouring"**: they sit next to copyright — derived from the same works, but not the same rights.

## Performer's rights: more than "the one who sings"

Performers' rights split in two as well:

- **Personal side**: to be identified as performer, to have the performance image protected from distortion;
- **Economic side**: to authorise live broadcast, recording, reproduction and distribution, network transmission — and be paid.

A common misunderstanding: **"the writer agreed, so any version of the singing is free" — not necessarily.** The work layer and the performance layer license separately (add the recording layer and it is three; see [how many rights live in one song](../copyright-composition/)).

## Producer's rights: the label's layer

Producers hold reproduction, distribution, rental and network-transmission rights in their recordings — **every audio file on a platform has this layer standing behind it.**

## Why everyone should know a little

- **Releasing a cover**: besides the work layer, using the original backing track (= the original recording) needs the recording layer too;
- **Reposting live footage**: may touch both performers and producers;
- **Remixing and sampling**: every layer must be cleared (see [sampling and transformation](../sampling-and-transform/)).

**One-line mnemonic: song, performance, recording — three layers, three owners; greets each in turn.**

## Listen: one material, a different rendering

The same melodic material, played by another person or processed differently, is another product — and that is exactly the layer neighbouring rights protect.
