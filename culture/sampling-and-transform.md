如果说「引用一句歌词」是远距离致敬，那么**采样就是贴身搬运**——你直接把一段**声音**（那一次演奏、那一次录音）搬进了自己的作品。它涉及的权利层最多、边界最绕，也最需要先把地图看清。

（权利结构介绍，不构成法律意见。）

## 采样踩到几层

- **录音层**（核心）：你搬的是**那份录音**——它的主人是录音制作者；
- **词曲层**：若采样的片段是"有旋律有词"的创作性内容，词曲层同样在；
- **表演者层**：那一次演奏的表演者，也可能主张自己的权利。

**所以「一段两秒钟的鼓点」听起来无害，却可能同时坐拥三位主人。**（见[一首歌上有几层权利](../copyright-composition/)）

## 两条路：清样与改写

1. **清样（clearance）**：找齐各层权利人，谈判好范围与费用，合法采样。大制作里的采样通常都走这条路——**"贵的不是那两秒，是不确定性"**；
2. **重录改写**：不用原录音，自己重奏那个片段或改写它——避开了录音层与表演层（但仍需处理词曲层，如果旋律被实质使用）。

## 关于「改到认不出」

一种流行说法是"改到别人认不出就没事"——**这不是法律标准**：
- "认不认出"是听感问题，"是否构成侵权"是法律判断；
- 判定通常看**实质性相似 + 接触可能**等要素的组合，而非"我改了 30%"这类数量口诀。

**唯一稳妥的边界，仍然是"清过样"或"自己重做"。**

## 数字时代的第三条路：一个干净的生态

今天有大量**为采样而生**的素材，明明白白解决了授权问题：

- **免版税/开放许可音色库**（条款清晰，常用 CC0）；
- **为采样设计的器乐素材与循环包**（购买即获许可）；
- **开放授权的数据集与素材网**（例如本站曲库中的开放许可音频——使用前依旧核对条款）。

**结论很朴素**：采样文化最繁荣的地方，恰恰是授权最清晰的地方。

If quoting a lyric is a distant salute, **sampling is a close embrace** — you move a piece of **sound** (that performance, that recording) into your own work. It touches the most layers and the most tangled boundaries, and deserves a map first.

(Rights structure, not legal advice.)

## How many layers a sample touches

- **Recording layer** (the core): you moved **the recording** — owned by its producer;
- **Work layer**: if the sampled bit carries melodic or lyrical content, the work layer is present too;
- **Performance layer**: performers in that take may assert their rights too.

**So an "harmless two-second drum hit" can sit on three owners at once.** (See [how many rights live in one song](../copyright-composition/).)

## Two roads: clearance and remake

1. **Clearance**: find every layer's owner, negotiate scope and fee, sample legally. Big productions usually walk this road — **"what costs is not the two seconds; it is the uncertainty";**
2. **Re-record or rewrite**: skip the original recording by replaying or rewriting — off the recording and performance layers (the work layer still applies if the melody is substantially used).

## About "changing it beyond recognition"

A popular claim says "change it enough and you are safe" — **not a legal standard**:
- Recognition is a listening question; infringement is a legal judgement;
- Tests usually combine **substantial similarity** with other factors — not "I changed 30%".

**The only sturdy boundaries remain: cleared, or re-made.**

## The third road of the digital age: a clean ecology

Today a whole supply exists **made for sampling**, with the licensing solved up front:

- **Royalty-free / openly licensed sound libraries** (clear terms, often CC0);
- **Instrument beds and loop packs built for sampling** (buy = licence);
- **Openly licensed datasets and material sources** (including the open-licensed audio in this site's library — still check terms before use).

**A plain conclusion**: sampling culture flourishes most where licensing is clearest.
