多数人学调式的方式是「背七套音阶」，结果一用就乱。**本页想让你换一种理解：调式不是七套音阶，是同一套音阶换七个主音。**

## 一个骨架，七个家

下面三组用的是**完全相同的七个音**（自然大调音阶），只是**主音（起点与终点）换了**。

## 这是三种，不是七种的原因

三个关键点：

1. **音列没变** —— 三组都是同样的七个音，只是排序起点不同；
2. **主音决定"家"** —— 你从哪个音开始、回到哪个音，它就是主音；
3. **调式的颜色由"哪个半音在哪里"决定** —— 而不是由七个音本身。

上面三组里：

- **C 爱奥尼亚**：半音在 E–F、B–C（都在中低位置）→ 平；
- **D 多利亚**：半音只在上方（B–C）→ 亮一点、带点"小调影子"；
- **F 利底亚**：升了四级（B 相对 F）→ 那种"向上飘、不落地"的悬浮感。

**所以要判断一个调式，你只需要问两件事：主音在哪 + 哪个音被抬高或压低。**

## 调式在什么地方真的有用

不是所有音乐都有调，但**调式是色彩最省力的一个旋钮**：

- **需要"暗但不悲"** —— 用多利亚（它的第六音是自然大，不要悲）；
- **需要"亮但不甜"** —— 用利底亚（升四级制造不稳定）；
- **需要"平原上开着花"的感觉** —— 混合利底亚与 Mixolydian。

**这些是"换一个音"的成本，就能得到很不一样的情绪。** 这是调式最实际的价值。

## 一个诚实的说明

本页只演示了三个调式。**但真实调式体系里不只有七个**——还有教会调式、混合调式、旋法（rotations）等。

**本页给的是"怎么想"的方法，不是"有哪些"的清单。** 掌握了"同骨架 + 换主音"这个思路，剩下的都是查表。

Most people learn modes by memorising seven scales, and then fall apart when they try to use one. **This page offers a different framing: modes are not seven scales; they are one scale with seven different tonics.**

## One frame, seven home bases

Below are **exactly the same seven notes** (the natural major scale), with only the **tonic — the note you start and end on — moved**.

## Why that is three modes rather than seven

Three key points:

1. **The note set never changes** — all three use the same seven notes, only the starting point differs;
2. **The tonic decides "home"** — whichever note you start and return to is the tonic;
3. **A mode's colour is decided by "where the semitone sits"** — not by the seven notes themselves.

Across the three above:

- **C aeolian**: semitones at E–F and B–C (both low) → plain;
- **D dorian**: the only semitone is at the top, B–C → brighter, with a shadow of minor;
- **F lydian**: raised fourth (B over F) → that floating, unresolved lift.

**So to identify a mode you only need two questions: where is the tonic, and which note has been raised or lowered?**

## Where modes are genuinely useful

Not all music has a mode, but **a mode is the most cost-effective colour knob there is**:

- **Wanting "dark but not sad"** — use dorian (its sixth degree comes from the natural major, so it resists pathos);
- **Wanting "bright but not sweet"** — use lydian (the raised fourth creates instability);
- **Wanting "flowers in a plain"** — mix lydian with mixolydian.

**Each of those is the cost of changing one note, and the emotional result is entirely different.** That is the real practical value of modes.

## An honest note

This page demonstrates three modes only. **Real modal systems contain more than seven** — there are church modes, hybrid modes, and rotations.

**What this page gives you is a way to think, not a list to memorise.** Once "same frame, different tonic" is understood, the rest is reference material.
