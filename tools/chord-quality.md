三和弦只有三种性质，但很多人听一辈子都分不清。**本页想告诉你：区别只有一个东西——中间那个音离根音多远。**

## 三种性质，各听一次

**第一种：大三和弦（根音上 0 / 4 / 7 个半音）**

## 关键：只有中间那个音在动

上面三组，**根音（C）和最高音（G 或 B♭）几乎没变**，变的是中间：

| 性质 | 根音 | 中间音 | 最高音 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 大 | C | **E**（+4） | G |
| 小 | C | **E♭**（+3） | G |
| 减 | C | **E♭**（+3） | **B♭**（+6） |

**这解释了为什么你能在钢琴上"看见"大小三和弦是同一个手型。** 因为它们真的是同一个手型，只是手指落的位置差半个键。

**而减三和弦不一样**——它的高音也降了（变成降七度）。**所以减三和弦听起来才那么"紧"。**

## 一个可自己验证的动作

1. 先只听第一组（大）的「分解」；
2. 再只听第二组（小）的「分解」；
3. **交替快速听**——你会发现那两个中间音（E 与 E♭）在你脑子里开始"对调"。

**这个"对调"的感觉就是听辨能力的建立。** 和本站音程那篇的方法完全一样。

## 三种性质分别在音乐里干什么

- **大三**：稳定的、开放的、开始或结束；
- **小三**：暗的、忧的、向下走的倾向；
- **减三**：**极不稳定**，它的作用是**要解决到别的地方去**。

**关键理解：减三和弦不是一种"色彩"，它是一个"问题"。** 它几乎总是出现在**要回家之前的那一刻**（属到主、导音到主）。

**所以当你听到减三和弦时，正确反应不是"这是什么颜色"，而是"它要去哪"。**

## 一个诚实的边界

本页只讲三和弦。**七和弦、挂留、加音这些都建立在三和弦之上**——理解了"中间那个音在动"这个思路，其余都是类似的加法。

There are only three triads, yet many people never manage to tell them apart. **The point of this page: the difference is one thing only — how far the middle note sits from the root.**

## The key: only the middle note moves

Across those three, **the root (C) and the top note barely change** — what moves is the middle:

| Quality | Root | Middle | Top |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major | C | **E** (+4) | G |
| Minor | C | **E♭** (+3) | G |
| Diminished | C | **E♭** (+3) | **B♭** (+6) |

**This is why you can "see" that major and minor triads are the same shape on the keyboard.** They genuinely are the same shape — the fingers just land half a key apart.

**Diminished is different** — its top note drops too (to the flattened seventh). **That is why diminished sounds so tight.**

## An action you can verify yourself

1. Listen only to the first (major) on "decompose";
2. Then only the second (minor) on "decompose";
3. **Alternate between them quickly** — you will feel those two middle notes (E and E♭) starting to swap places in your mind.

**That "swapping" sensation *is* the build-up of identification ability.** The method is identical to the interval pages here.

## What each quality does in music

- **Major**: settled, open, a beginning or an ending;
- **Minor**: darker, worried, a downward pull;
- **Diminished**: **highly unstable** — its job is to **resolve somewhere else**.

**The key understanding: a diminished triad is not a "colour". It is a "question".** It almost always appears on the beat before going home (dominant to tonic, leading tone to tonic).

**So when you hear a diminished triad, the right reaction is not "what colour is this" but "where is it going".**

## An honest boundary

This page covers triads only. **Sevenths, suspensions and added notes all build on the triad** — once you have the idea of "the middle note moves", the rest is addition.
