::: zh
「想用 AI 出音乐，生成几次之后发现拿不出手。」这条路的重点不是工具本身，而是把生成结果变成能交付的东西：选得对、改得动、说得清权利。

## 这条路径适合谁

适合要快速拿到可用音频的人：短视频、Demo、游戏占位、批量配乐。也适合先看清权利边界再接活的人。不适合想练手上功夫的人——那是[学一件乐器](../path-instrument/)。

## 先读这三页

- [工具地图](https://ai.midicn.com/ai-music-tool-map/) —— AI 做音乐是一串小工具，各管一段；先知道自己在哪一段。
- [提示词结构](https://ai.midicn.com/prompt-anatomy/) —— 风格、编制、速度、情绪、结构，提示写得像编曲说明，结果才可控。
- [评估生成结果](https://ai.midicn.com/evaluating-generations/) —— 会挑比会生成重要：先听结构，最后才听混音。

## 接下来

- [选型方法](https://ai.midicn.com/choosing-a-tool/) —— 别问哪个最好，先写清交付物与控制需求。
- [从想法到母带](https://ai.midicn.com/workflow-from-idea-to-master/) —— 一条可重复的流水线。
- [AI 输出与版权](https://ai.midicn.com/copyright-of-ai-output/) —— 机器生成的不一定归你，人的选择才是可主张的部分。
- [何时不该用 AI](https://ai.midicn.com/when-not-to-use-ai/) —— 有些活用真人更快或根本不能生成。
- [AI 音乐导论](https://prod.midicn.com/ai-music-introduction/) —— 符号与音频两条路的区别。

## 可以跳过

系统乐理、配器、模拟录音与话筒摆位、母带链路。先跑通一条能重复的流水线，比补齐知识更快见效。

## 常见走弯路

1. 追新工具，不建流程。换工具的成本高于写清自己的交付物。
2. 一条提示定生死。同一需求至少做五个变体再挑。
3. 不问授权就商用。生成结果是起点，不是权利证明。

::: en
You want music out of AI, and after a few generations it still does not hold up. This path is less about tools than about turning a generation into a deliverable: choose well, edit what you got, and be able to state the rights.

## Who this path is for

For anyone who needs usable audio fast: short video, demos, game placeholders, bulk background music. Also for anyone who wants the rights picture straight before taking work. Not for building technique — that is [Learning an Instrument](../path-instrument/).

## Read these three first

- [The tool map](https://ai.midicn.com/ai-music-tool-map/) — AI music is a chain of small tools, each covering one segment; know which segment you are in.
- [Prompt anatomy](https://ai.midicn.com/prompt-anatomy/) — style, instrumentation, tempo, mood, structure; write the prompt like an arrangement brief and the output becomes controllable.
- [Evaluating generations](https://ai.midicn.com/evaluating-generations/) — choosing well matters more than generating; hear structure first, mix last.

## What comes next

- [Choosing a tool](https://ai.midicn.com/choosing-a-tool/) — do not ask which is best; write down the deliverable and the control you need.
- [From idea to master](https://ai.midicn.com/workflow-from-idea-to-master/) — one repeatable pipeline.
- [Copyright of AI output](https://ai.midicn.com/copyright-of-ai-output/) — machine output is not automatically yours; your choices are what you can claim.
- [When not to use AI](https://ai.midicn.com/when-not-to-use-ai/) — some jobs are faster with people, or cannot be generated at all.
- [Introduction to AI music](https://prod.midicn.com/ai-music-introduction/) — symbolic and audio routes solve different problems.

## What you can skip

Systematic theory, orchestration, analogue recording and microphone placement, the mastering chain. A repeatable pipeline pays off sooner than filling knowledge gaps.

## Common detours

1. Chasing new tools instead of building a workflow. Switching costs more than specifying your deliverable.
2. One prompt decides everything. Make five variants of the same brief before choosing.
3. Going commercial without asking about licensing. A generation is a starting point, not proof of rights.
