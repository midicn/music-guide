::: zh
midicn 提供音乐知识、创作方法与音乐资料的查阅入口。

- [学音乐](../learn/)：从读谱、节奏、乐器和听赏进入具体条目。
- [做音乐](../make/)：按写作、编曲、录音、混音与交付找到资料。
- [MIDI 库](https://lib.midicn.com/)与[音色库](https://sf.midicn.com/)：查看文件、试听及逐项来源许可。
- [音乐导航](https://nav.midicn.com/)：浏览本站分站与音乐外站。

知识内容在乐理、乐器、音乐史、音乐词典、音频制作和 AI 音乐等分站展开。可以用[全站搜索](../search/)查关键词，或看[站点与内容导航](../hubs/hub-sites-map/)。

页脚广告支持网站运行。本站自有内容与第三方材料的使用条件不同，详见[版权说明](../legal/copyright/)和[数据使用条款](../legal/data/terms/)；数据处理见[隐私政策](../legal/privacy/)。

发现错误，请到[项目反馈页](https://github.com/midicn/home/issues)提供页面地址、原句和依据。正文中的例子、试听和练习用于帮助理解，不能代替对作品、设备文档及来源许可的核对。

::: en
midicn provides reference material for music study, creative work and music resources.

- [Learn music](../learn/): enter topics through notation, rhythm, instruments and listening.
- [Create music](../make/): find material on writing, arranging, recording, mixing and delivery.
- [MIDI Library](https://lib.midicn.com/) and [SoundFonts](https://sf.midicn.com/): browse files, listen and check their source licences.
- [Music Directory](https://nav.midicn.com/): explore midicn sites and music websites.

Theory, instruments, history, the glossary, production and AI music are covered on their respective sites. Use [site-wide search](../search/) or the [site and content guide](../hubs/hub-sites-map/) to find a topic.

Footer advertising supports operation. Site-owned explanations and third-party materials have different terms: see [content rights](../legal/copyright/), [data terms](../legal/data/terms/) and the [privacy policy](../legal/privacy/).

To report an error, use the [project issue page](https://github.com/midicn/home/issues) and include the page, disputed wording and evidence. Examples, playback and exercises support learning; they do not replace checking works, device documentation and source licences.
