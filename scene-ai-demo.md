::: zh
Demo 的用途是让别人听懂你的歌大概长什么样。**它不需要发行级，但必须结构完整、主旋律能哼出来。**

## 你要交的东西

一条两到三分钟的完整小样：有主歌、副歌、桥段，有能记住的旋律，附一段说明用了什么工具、改了什么。给合作者听，不是给平台听。

## 三步走完

1. 先写 brief：风格、编制、速度、情绪、结构，像给编曲的说明，而不是一句"来首好听的"。
2. 分段生成再拼装，别指望一次生成整首还对得上结构。
3. 人工修：剪结构、换段落、重录人声或换主奏，把"AI 味"最重的那一层换掉。

## 用到的页面

- [提示词解剖](https://ai.midicn.com/prompt-anatomy/) —— 好的提示像编曲说明，五个要素缺一不可。
- [分段生成](https://ai.midicn.com/section-by-section-generation/) —— 一段一段出，结构才受你控制。
- [评估生成结果](https://ai.midicn.com/evaluating-generations/) —— 先听结构，最后才听混音，别被音色骗。
- [从想法到成品的工作流](https://ai.midicn.com/workflow-from-idea-to-master/) —— 可重复的流水线比任何单点技巧可靠。
- [什么时候不该用 AI](https://ai.midicn.com/when-not-to-use-ai/) —— 有些活真人更快、更好，或者根本不能生成。

## 什么算合格

- 能标出主歌、副歌、桥段的位置，总长 2–3 分钟，结构不和歌词打架。
- 主旋律能被别人听一遍后哼出来，副歌出现不少于两次。
- 交付时附工具、版本、提示词与人工改动清单，别人能据此复现。
:::

::: en
A demo exists so someone can hear what the song is. **It does not need release quality, but it must have a complete structure and a hummable melody.**

## What you hand in

A complete sketch of two to three minutes: verses, chorus, bridge and a memorable melody, plus a note on what tools you used and what you changed. It is for collaborators, not for a platform.

## Three moves

1. Write the brief first: style, instrumentation, tempo, mood, form — an arrangement note, not "make something good".
2. Generate section by section and assemble; do not expect one pass to follow your structure.
3. Edit by hand: cut the form, swap sections, re-record vocals or replace the lead so the most synthetic layer is gone.

## The pages you need

- [Prompt anatomy](https://ai.midicn.com/prompt-anatomy/) — a good prompt reads like an arrangement brief; all five elements matter.
- [Section-by-section generation](https://ai.midicn.com/section-by-section-generation/) — generate in parts if you want control of the form.
- [Evaluating generations](https://ai.midicn.com/evaluating-generations/) — form first, mix last; do not be fooled by timbre.
- [Workflow from idea to master](https://ai.midicn.com/workflow-from-idea-to-master/) — a repeatable pipeline beats any single trick.
- [When not to use AI](https://ai.midicn.com/when-not-to-use-ai/) — some jobs are faster, better or only legal with a human.

## What counts as good

- Verses, chorus and bridge can be marked, total length 2–3 minutes, and the form does not fight the lyric.
- Someone can hum the melody after one listen, and the chorus appears at least twice.
- Hand over tool, version, prompt and a list of manual edits so the result can be reproduced.
:::
