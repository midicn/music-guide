::: zh
片头会被听几十次，问题不是"好不好听"，而是"听第五十次烦不烦"。

## 你要交的东西

一条 15 秒或 30 秒的片头音乐，接在主播开口之前，**结尾必须给人声留出一个干净入口**。它不是一首歌，是每期重复的一个动作。

## 分三步做

1. 定两个版本（15 秒与 30 秒）和最后的落点：音乐停在哪一拍，主播第一句才不打架。
2. 只留一个记忆点——三五颗音的动机，重复或变形，不要在 30 秒里塞三个主意。
3. 做电平匹配：片头与口播的响度差控制在 2 LU 内，别让听众每期拧音量。

## 用到的页面

- [引子、过渡与尾奏](https://prod.midicn.com/intro-transition-and-outro/) —— 引子只介绍一个元素，30 秒最容易违反这条。
- [钩子设计](https://prod.midicn.com/hook-design/) —— 记忆点要短、可重复、可变形，三次才站得住。
- [平台响度目标](https://prod.midicn.com/platform-loudness-targets/) —— 播客平台会归一化，按错目标做等于白做。
- [短视频音乐](https://ai.midicn.com/music-for-short-form/) —— 短时长、强开头、可循环，和片头的结构要求几乎一样。
- [响度归一化](../culture/loudness-normalization/) —— 为什么平台会改你的音量，怎么提前适应。

## 什么算合格

- 时长正好 15.0 或 30.0 秒，结尾无混响尾巴压住第一句话。
- 片头与口播响度差不超过 2 LU，整体约 −16 LUFS（播客常见目标）。
- 交付 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV 与一条 MP3 试听版，文件名含期数与版本。
:::

::: en
An intro is heard dozens of times, so the question is not "is it good" but "is it tolerable on the fiftieth listen".

## What you hand in

A 15- or 30-second theme that plays before the host speaks, **with a clean entry point for the voice at the end**. It is not a song; it is a fixed action repeated every episode.

## Do it in three steps

1. Fix two versions (15 s and 30 s) and the final landing: which beat the music stops on so the first sentence does not fight it.
2. Keep one hook — a three-to-five-note motif, repeated or varied. Do not cram three ideas into 30 seconds.
3. Match levels: keep the gap between intro and speech under 2 LU so listeners never reach for the volume knob.

## The pages you need

- [Intro, transition and outro](https://prod.midicn.com/intro-transition-and-outro/) — an intro introduces one element; that is the rule 30-second themes break most.
- [Hook design](https://prod.midicn.com/hook-design/) — short, repeatable, transformable, and it needs three appearances to stick.
- [Platform loudness targets](https://prod.midicn.com/platform-loudness-targets/) — podcast platforms normalise; targeting the wrong level wastes the work.
- [Music for short form](https://ai.midicn.com/music-for-short-form/) — short, front-loaded and loopable, almost the same structural brief as an intro.
- [Loudness normalisation](../culture/loudness-normalization/) — why platforms change your level, and how to plan for it.

## What counts as good

- Exactly 15.0 or 30.0 seconds, with no reverb tail covering the first sentence.
- Intro and speech within 2 LU of each other, overall around −16 LUFS, a common podcast target.
- Deliver 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV plus an MP3 audition, filename carrying episode number and version.
:::
