## 你现在大概会什么

有客户或固定合作方，能按 deadline 交付，同时跑几个项目。工具很熟，但流程装在脑子里，换个人接手就乱；报价靠感觉，改稿没有上限。

## 最该补的一件事

把交付变成文档：格式、采样率、位深、响度、分轨命名、修改轮次，写成模板与检查表。这一步决定你能接多少活，而不只是能做好多少活。模板一旦成型，新项目就从「从零搭」变成「填空」。

## 三页起步

- [母带交付格式](https://prod.midicn.com/master-delivery-formats/) —— 格式、采样率、位深与余量写清楚，省掉一半返工。
- [工程交换格式](https://prod.midicn.com/session-interchange-formats/) —— 换 DAW 或交后期时，能带走的通常只有时间与音量。
- [AI 合同清单](https://ai.midicn.com/contract-checklist/) —— 范围、披露、权利、修改与退出，一条条对。

## 暂时别碰

入门心态与天赋讨论、基础乐理的系统复习、新工具尝鲜。前两件按需查，第三件等流程稳定再说。

## 怎么判断自己进阶了

- 改稿轮次下降，报价能一次说清范围。
- 交付返工为零，别人照你的清单就能接手。
- 项目并行时，你能把其中一段完整交给别人做。

## What you can probably do now

You have clients or regular collaborators, you deliver to deadlines and run several projects at once. The tools are familiar, but the process lives in your head, and it jams the moment someone else takes over. Quotes are guesses and revisions have no ceiling.

## The one thing to fix

Turn delivery into a document: format, sample rate, bit depth, loudness, stem naming, revision rounds — as templates and checklists. This decides how much work you can take, not just how well you can do it. Once a template exists, a new project stops being a build and becomes a fill-in.

## Three pages to start

- [Master delivery formats](https://prod.midicn.com/master-delivery-formats/) — write format, sample rate, bit depth and headroom down and half the rework disappears.
- [Session interchange formats](https://prod.midicn.com/session-interchange-formats/) — when changing DAWs or handing over, usually only time and level travel with you.
- [AI contract checklist](https://ai.midicn.com/contract-checklist/) — scope, disclosure, rights, revisions and exit, item by item.

## Do not touch yet

Beginner mindset and talent debates, systematic revision of basic theory, trying every new tool. Look the first two up as needed; save the third until the workflow is stable.

## How you know you have moved up

- Revision rounds drop, and a quote states its scope in one pass.
- Deliverables come back with zero rework; someone else can take over from your checklist.
- With projects running in parallel, you can hand one section over complete.
