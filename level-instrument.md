## 你现在大概会什么

能弹几首完整的曲子，看得懂简单谱，练琴靠模仿录音或老师示范。换调、换风格、换一首没听过的曲子，速度立刻掉下来。

## 最该补的一件事

把手上的动作接到听得出、说得出上：音阶与和弦在琴上的位置，和它们在耳朵里的样子，要能对上号。这一条通了，练琴才不是背动作。先从一首已经在弹的曲子开始，把它的调、和弦位置与旋律走向说清楚，比再记十个术语有用。

## 三页起步

- [音程](https://theo.midicn.com/interval/) —— 和声与音阶的最小单位，所有「听出来」都从距离开始。
- [怎么练才有效](../start/deliberate-practice/) —— 定义问题、缩小范围、立刻反馈。
- [背谱与记忆](../start/memorization/) —— 给音乐多条索引，上台才不断。

## 暂时别碰

重属与增六和弦、配器与管弦乐法、混音母带、AI 生成。现在缺的是把已经会的东西连起来，不是更多新名词。

## 怎么判断自己进阶了

- 拿到新谱，先读节奏再读音，不靠听录音找位置。
- 能听出一段旋律里的大跳落在哪一句。
- 两周内把一首曲子弹到不断、能停、能重来。

## What you can probably do now

You can play a few pieces end to end, read simple notation, and you learn by copying a recording or a teacher's demonstration. Change the key, the style, or the piece, and speed collapses.

## The one thing to fix

Connect the hands to the ear and the mouth: where scales and chords sit on the instrument must match how they sound. Once that lines up, practice stops being memorised motion. Start with a piece you already play: name its key, its chord positions and where the melody goes.

## Three pages to start

- [Interval](https://theo.midicn.com/interval/) — the smallest unit of harmony and scales; everything you hear starts with distance.
- [How to practise so it works](../start/deliberate-practice/) — define the problem, narrow the range, get feedback at once.
- [Memorisation](../start/memorization/) — give the music several indexes so it holds on stage.

## Do not touch yet

Secondary dominants and augmented sixths, orchestration, mixing and mastering, AI generation. What is missing is connecting what you already have, not more new nouns.

## How you know you have moved up

- With a new score you read rhythm first, then pitch, without hunting for the recording.
- You can hear which phrase a large leap lands in.
- Within two weeks a piece plays through without stopping, and you can stop and restart it at will.
