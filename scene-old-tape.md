::: zh
老磁带的底噪、抖晃和掉磁是物理事实，不是 bug。**修得越狠，原声丢得越多**，功夫全在"停在哪里"。

## 做出来的是什么

一份能听、能留存的数字化文件，通常还有采集备份。不是"修复到像新录的"，而是把听不下去的部分变成能听，同时保住人声质感。

## 三步走完

1. 按 48 kHz / 24-bit 数字化，采集阶段不做任何降噪，先留最原始的版本。
2. 把问题分三类：嘶声、嗡声、咔嗒与掉音，一类一种处理，别用一条链全压。
3. 逐段 A/B，宁可留一点底噪，也不要让人声出现"水下感"或泵动。

- [噪声与嗡声](https://prod.midicn.com/noise-and-hum/) —— 嘶声来自电路、哼声来自接地，分不清就修不好。

## 什么算合格

- 修复前后 A/B 明显可辨，但人声不"塑料"、无泵动与金属味。
- 底噪下降至少 10 dB，且没有整段忽大忽小的呼吸感。
- 交付 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV，另存一份未处理的采集原始文件。

::: en
Hiss, wow and dropouts on an old tape are physical facts, not software bugs. **The harder you clean, the more of the original you lose**, so the skill here is knowing where to stop.

## What gets made

A listenable, archivable digital file, usually alongside the raw capture. Not "restored to sound new", but turning the unlistenable parts into listenable ones while keeping the voice intact.

## Three moves

1. Digitise at 48 kHz / 24-bit with no noise reduction during capture, and keep the untouched version.
2. Sort the problems into three kinds — hiss, hum, clicks and dropouts — and treat each with its own tool instead of one long chain.
3. A/B section by section; leave a little noise rather than giving the voice an underwater or pumping quality.

## The pages you need

- [Audio restoration](https://prod.midicn.com/audio-restoration/) — what noise reduction, hum removal and click repair each cost; read this before touching anything.
- [Noise and hum](https://prod.midicn.com/noise-and-hum/) — hiss comes from the circuit, hum from grounding; you cannot fix what you cannot name.
- [Fixing artefacts](https://ai.midicn.com/fixing-artifacts/) — decide whether it is an artefact or a performance choice before repairing.
- [Audio file formats](https://prod.midicn.com/audio-file-formats/) — what to archive in, and when to make a lossy copy.
- [Recording history](../culture/recording-history/) — tape never had modern bandwidth; do not judge it by digital standards.

## What counts as good

- The before/after A/B is clearly audible, yet the voice is not plasticky and shows no pumping or metallic edge.
- Noise floor drops by at least 10 dB with no section-to-section breathing.
- Deliver 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV and keep the unprocessed capture alongside it.
