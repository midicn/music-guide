::: zh
「画面已经在了，需要音乐替它说时间。」这条路按秒和情绪曲线工作，不按小节；它是叙事的一部分，不是贴上去的装饰。

## 这条路径适合谁

适合手上有成片、关卡或分镜的人；也适合想接商业片子的作曲者。不适合先写完歌再找画面的人——先去[写一首歌](../path-write/)。

## 先读这三页

- [影视配乐导论](https://prod.midicn.com/film-scoring-introduction/) —— 音乐是叙事的第二层，先摆正它和画面的关系。
- [音画关系](https://prod.midicn.com/music-picture-sync/) —— 同步点、对位与反差，决定音乐跟着画面走还是与画面顶撞。
- [配乐工作流程](https://prod.midicn.com/scoring-workflow/) —— 读本、spotting、写 cue、混录；流程比等灵感可靠。

## 接下来

- [游戏音频导论](https://prod.midicn.com/game-audio-introduction/) —— 游戏音乐是被触发和拼接的素材系统，不是一条音轨。
- [主题动机](https://prod.midicn.com/leitmotif-in-film/) —— 一个短动机标记人物，靠变形而不是换材料叙事。
- [情绪曲线](https://prod.midicn.com/emotional-arc/) —— 张力与回落，不是一路渐强。
- [临时音轨与参考策略](https://prod.midicn.com/temp-track-strategy/) —— 临时轨暴露需求，也容易困住原创。
- [配乐交付与分轨](https://prod.midicn.com/score-delivery-and-stems/) —— 交付不只是立体声文件。
- [给视频配乐：同步权的实操流程](../culture/sync-license/) —— 用别人的曲子要先清权。

## 可以跳过

主歌副歌的写作法、母带与响度优化、AI 生成细节、管弦乐配器的深入内容。先用采样把 cue 写出来，配器可以后补。

## 常见走弯路

1. 先写完三分钟再对画面。配乐的长度由画面决定。
2. 每个镜头都换音乐。换得太勤，观众什么都记不住。
3. 只交一个立体声。分轨命名与响度同样是交付内容。

::: en
The picture already exists and needs music to tell its time. This path works in seconds and emotional arcs, not bars; music is a layer of the narrative, not decoration stuck on top.

## Who this path is for

For anyone holding a cut, a level or a storyboard; also for composers aiming at paid work. Not for writing the song first and hunting for footage later — go to [Writing a Song](../path-write/).

## Read these three first

- [Introduction to film scoring](https://prod.midicn.com/film-scoring-introduction/) — music is the second layer of narrative; settle its relation to picture first.
- [Music and picture](https://prod.midicn.com/music-picture-sync/) — sync points, counterpoint and contrast decide whether music follows the image or collides with it.
- [The scoring workflow](https://prod.midicn.com/scoring-workflow/) — spotting, writing cues, recording and mixing; process beats waiting for inspiration.

## What comes next

- [Introduction to game audio](https://prod.midicn.com/game-audio-introduction/) — game music is a system of triggered and stitched assets, not one fixed track.
- [Leitmotif](https://prod.midicn.com/leitmotif-in-film/) — one short motive marks a character and narrates by transformation, not by new material.
- [Emotional arc](https://prod.midicn.com/emotional-arc/) — tension and release, not a long crescendo.
- [Temp tracks and reference strategy](https://prod.midicn.com/temp-track-strategy/) — a temp track reveals the brief and can trap the original.
- [Score delivery and stems](https://prod.midicn.com/score-delivery-and-stems/) — delivery is more than a stereo file.
- [Sync licensing in practice](../culture/sync-license/) — using someone else's track starts with clearing rights.

## What you can skip

Verse-chorus songwriting, mastering and loudness polishing, AI generation detail, deep orchestration. Write the cue with samples first; orchestration can follow.

## Common detours

1. Writing three minutes first, then fitting picture. Picture decides length.
2. New music for every shot. Change that often and the audience remembers nothing.
3. Delivering one stereo file. Stem naming and loudness are part of delivery too.
