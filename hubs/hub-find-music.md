「我想找曲子」有两种完全不同的意思：**想听一遍**，和**想拿去做**。**这两种的路径、代价、注意事项都不一样**，混在一起是大多数麻烦的来源。

## 你在这里想做什么

先选一条：

- **A：我想听** —— 目标是找一首能听的
- B：我要用 —— 目标是把文件放进自己的项目里
- C：我要查 —— 目标是想弄清某个作品/作曲家/来源的准确信息

选 B 之前请务必读完「许可」那一节。 那部分不是套话，会直接影响你能不能发布。

## 去哪读：三条路

### 路 A：想听

- [数据集总览](https://lib.midicn.com/) —— 全部曲目的来源、许可与下载入口
- [曲目详情](https://mid.midicn.com/) —— 按作品查
- 站内：[中国音乐市场：一条自己的路径](../../culture/chinese-music-market/) —— 怎么按来源找
- 站内：[音乐产业地图：钱从哪来](../../culture/music-industry-map/) —— 先看音乐是怎么被组织起来的

### 路 B：要用（放进自己的项目）

这一路的规矩全在许可里，所以先读这三篇：

- [一首歌上有几层权利](../../culture/copyright-composition/) —— 最基础的一篇：同一首歌上不止一层权利
- [知识共享（CC）许可家族：六个组合怎么读](../../culture/cc-license-family/) —— C1/C2/C3 的区别就在这里
- [开放音乐数据与许可：数据集怎么合法](../../culture/open-music-data-license/) —— 数据集该怎么合法用

取数据的地方：

- [数据集总览](https://lib.midicn.com/) —— 逐曲标注许可，不改变原许可
- [压缩包](https://zip.midicn.com/) —— 打包下载
- [音频](https://sf.midicn.com/) —— 音频与采样资源

### 路 C：要查

- [曲目详情](https://mid.midicn.com/) —— 按作品查年代、作者、编制
- 站外：[作曲家](https://comp.midicn.com/) · [音乐史](https://hist.midicn.com/) —— 两站仍是骨架状态
- 站外：[音乐词典](https://gloss.midicn.com/) —— 术语（内容量不足）

## 许可的三档，你只需要记住一件事

站内所有内容都用同一套四档标注（C1 / C2 / C3 与公有领域），核心区别是：

| 档 | 你能做什么 |
|---|---|
| **C1** | 可商用、可改编，**须署名** |
| **C2** | **非商业**用途，可改编，须署名 |
| **C3** | **仅学习研究**，须署名 |
| **公有领域** | 无署名要求 |

「须署名」是所有档位的共同底线。 具体每首是什么档，看数据页的标注。

⚠️ 本站不改变原许可。 如果某个来源要求更严格的条款，以来源的声明为准——这一点在[开放音乐数据与许可](../../culture/open-music-data-license/)里有完整说明。

## 一个容易犯的错

"能下载"不等于"能用"。 下载是技术动作，授权是法律事实。两者是分开的。

站内有一整节专门讲这个误区：[版权常见误区：十条说法十条回应](../../culture/copyright-myths/)。

## 还没建好的

四处诚实说明：

1. 曲目详情站还没建好 —— 你现在只能在数据集总览里按标题搜，不能按作曲家、朝代、体裁检索
2. 没有"按难度/场合推荐"的曲目表 —— 数据集有 13 万多首，但没有筛选入口
3. 作曲家与音乐史站仍在骨架 —— "查准确信息"这条路现在只能靠外部来源
4. 词典站不足以支撑查证 —— 术语定义应该以它为准，现在还是骨架

第 1 与第 2 点是真缺口：13 万首数据没有检索层，等于"有一座仓库但没有货架"。

"I want to find a piece" means two entirely different things: **I want to listen to it**, and **I want to use the file**. **The routes, the costs and the cautions differ.** Mixing them up is the usual source of trouble.

## What you are trying to do here

Pick one:

- **A: I want to listen** — the goal is one piece to hear
- B: I want to use it — the goal is putting the file into your own project
- C: I want to look it up — the goal is accurate information about a work, composer or source

If you pick B, read the licence section before going further. It is not boilerplate; it directly affects whether you can publish.

## Where to read: three routes

### Route A: I want to listen

- [Dataset overview](https://lib.midicn.com/) — sources, licences and download entries
- [Track details](https://mid.midicn.com/) — look up a work
- Onsite: [the Chinese music market's own path](../../culture/chinese-music-market/) — how to browse by source
- Onsite: [the music industry map: where the money is](../../culture/music-industry-map/) — see how music is organised

### Route B: I want to use it (in my own project)

The rules on this route are all in the licences, so read these three first:

- [How many rights sit on one song](../../culture/copyright-composition/) — the most basic piece: one song carries more than one layer of rights
- [The CC licence family: reading six combinations](../../culture/cc-license-family/) — the C1/C2/C3 difference is here
- [Open music data and licensing](../../culture/open-music-data-license/) — how to use a dataset legally

Where to get the data:

- [Dataset overview](https://lib.midicn.com/) — per-track licensing; we do not alter the original terms
- [Archives](https://zip.midicn.com/) — packaged downloads
- [Audio](https://sf.midicn.com/) — audio and sample resources

### Route C: I want to look it up

- [Track details](https://mid.midicn.com/) — era, composer, scoring per work
- Offsite: [composers](https://comp.midicn.com/) · [music history](https://hist.midicn.com/) — both still skeletons
- Offsite: [glossary](https://gloss.midicn.com/) — terms (short of its plan)

## The licence tiers: one thing to remember

Everything here uses the same four-tier labelling (C1 / C2 / C3 and public domain). The differences:

| Tier | What you may do |
|---|---|
| **C1** | commercial use and adaptation, **with attribution** |
| **C2** | **non-commercial** use and adaptation, with attribution |
| **C3** | **study and research only**, with attribution |
| **Public domain** | no attribution required |

"With attribution" is the common floor across all tiers. What tier each track falls into is stated on its data page.

⚠️ This site does not alter the original licence. Where a source demands stricter terms, the source's own statement governs — [open music data and licensing](../../culture/open-music-data-license/) explains this in full.

## A common mistake

"I can download it" does not mean "I may use it". Downloading is a technical act; authorisation is a legal fact. They are separate.

A whole section exists for that misconception: [copyright myths: ten claims, ten answers](../../culture/copyright-myths/).

## What is not built yet

Four honest notes:

1. The track-details site is not built — you can currently only search the dataset overview by title, not by composer, period or genre
2. There is no "recommended by difficulty or occasion" list — the dataset holds over 133,000 works, but there is no filter to narrow them
3. The composer and music-history sites are still skeletons — the "look it up accurately" route currently depends on external sources
4. The glossary is not sufficient for verification — term definitions should come from it, and it is still a skeleton

Points 1 and 2 are genuine gaps: 133,000 works with no retrieval layer is a warehouse without shelving.
