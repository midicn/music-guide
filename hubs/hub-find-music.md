先确定要试听、查资料，还是下载文件用于自己的项目，再选择入口。

## 找曲目与试听

- [MIDI 音乐库](https://lib.midicn.com/)：按关键词搜索，查看曲目详情并试听。
- [维度浏览](https://mid.midicn.com/)：按作曲家、地区、流派、时期、乐器与来源查找。
- [音乐史](https://hist.midicn.com/)：了解作品所处的时期、地区与音乐背景。
- [音乐词典](https://gloss.midicn.com/)：查询相关术语、人物与体裁。

## 取文件与音色

- 单曲 MIDI：从[音乐库](https://lib.midicn.com/)进入该曲目的详情页。
- 下载包：[打包下载](https://zip.midicn.com/)。
- SoundFont：[音色库](https://sf.midicn.com/)。

下载前查看对应曲目或音色的来源与许可；可下载不等于适合所有用途。具体说明见[曲库许可](https://lib.midicn.com/licenses.html)，不要把目录标签当作原始许可文本。

## 找到之后怎么用

想编辑 MIDI、安排音色或做混音，可从主站[做音乐](../../make/)进入具体步骤。曲库的筛选维度不等同于按个人演奏水平编排的推荐课程。

Decide whether you want to listen, look something up or download a file for a project, then choose a destination.

## Find tracks and listen

- [MIDI library](https://lib.midicn.com/): search keywords, open track details and listen.
- [Dimensions](https://mid.midicn.com/): browse by composer, region, genre, period, instrument or source.
- [Music history](https://hist.midicn.com/en/): explore the period, region and musical context.
- [Glossary](https://gloss.midicn.com/en/): look up related terms, people and genres.

## Get files and sounds

- Individual MIDI files: open a track's detail page in the [library](https://lib.midicn.com/).
- Download collections: [Packs](https://zip.midicn.com/).
- SoundFonts: [SoundFont library](https://sf.midicn.com/).

Check the source and licence for the specific track or SoundFont before using it. A download does not grant every possible use. Read the [library licence information](https://lib.midicn.com/licenses.html); directory labels do not replace the original licence text.

## Work with what you found

Use [Make music](../../make/) to find steps for editing MIDI, choosing sounds or mixing. Browsing dimensions are not a course graded for an individual's playing level.
