「想学音乐，但不知道从哪下手」——这一页给你三条路，**选一条走就行，不要三条一起看。**

## 你在这里想做什么

先回答一个问题：**你现在最卡在哪？** 三条路对应三个不同的卡点。

## 去哪读：三条路

### 路线 A：先决定「要不要学、往哪学」

**走这条，如果你连"学什么乐器"都没想好。**

- [从零开始学音乐，第一步做什么](../../start/first-steps/) —— 先定方向、选一条起跑线
- [成年了，还能学音乐吗](../../start/adult-beginner/) —— 成年人的优势与诚实的障碍
- [「我有没有天赋」：一个问错的问题](../../start/talent-myth/) —— 为什么这问题没法回答
- [先学乐理，还是先练琴](../../start/theory-or-instrument-first/) —— 它们不是排队关系

**这一路全程在 [MIDI音乐 首页](https://home.midicn.com/start/) 里，共三十篇短文，每篇只回答一个问题。**

### 路线 B：已经决定学乐器了，卡在"怎么开始练"

走这条，如果你已经选了乐器或 vocal，但不知道怎么练。

- [每天该练多久](../../start/daily-practice-time/) —— 频率比时长重要
- [先学乐理，还是先练琴](../../start/theory-or-instrument-first/) —— 练琴与乐理可以并行
- 工具：[拍号：3/4 与 4/4 听起来为什么不一样](../../tools/rhythm-metre/) —— 可以直接点着听
- 站外：[乐器图鉴](https://inst.midicn.com/) —— 先了解你要学的那件乐器怎么发声

### 路线 C：想搞懂"音乐到底是什么"

走这条，如果你更想"理解"而不是"上手"。

- [音乐产业地图：钱从哪来](../../culture/music-industry-map/) —— 先看音乐怎么被组织起来
- [大调为什么听起来「快乐」：解释与争议](../../culture/major-happy-why/) —— 从一个最简单的现象开始
- 站外：[音乐理论](https://theo.midicn.com/) —— 一百五十余条理论条目

## 接着怎么学

遇到术语可以查[音乐词典](https://gloss.midicn.com/)。想把基础知识与练习连起来，可从主站[学音乐](../../learn/)选择起步路径，再进入乐器站阅读对应内容。导览不能代替针对具体乐器的练习安排。

"I want to learn music but do not know where to start" — three routes follow. **Pick one; do not read all three.**

## What you are trying to do here

Answer one question first: **what is actually blocking you?** The three routes match three different blockers.

## Where to read: three routes

### Route A: decide whether and what to learn

**Take this if you have not even chosen an instrument.**

- [Starting from zero: the first step](../../start/first-steps/) — set a direction first
- [Is it too late as an adult?](../../start/adult-beginner/) — real advantages and real obstacles
- ["Do I have talent?": a misframed question](../../start/talent-myth/) — why this question has no answer
- [Theory first, or instrument first?](../../start/theory-or-instrument-first/) — they are not a queue

**This route lives entirely in the [MIDI Music home site](https://home.midicn.com/start/): thirty short pieces, one question each.**

### Route B: you have chosen an instrument but not how to practise

**Take this if you have picked an instrument but do not know how to practise.**

- [How long should I practise each day?](../../start/daily-practice-time/) — frequency beats duration
- [Theory first, or instrument first?](../../start/theory-or-instrument-first/) — the two can run in parallel
- Tool: [metre: why 3/4 and 4/4 sound different](../../tools/rhythm-metre/) — clickable
- Offsite: [the instrument guide](https://inst.midicn.com/) — understand how your instrument makes sound first

### Route C: you want to understand what music is

Take this if you would rather understand than start playing.

- [The music industry map: where the money is](../../culture/music-industry-map/) — see how music is organised
- [Why the major scale sounds happy: explanation and dispute](../../culture/major-happy-why/) — start from the simplest phenomenon
- Offsite: [music theory](https://theo.midicn.com/) — over 150 theory entries

## Where to continue

Look up unfamiliar terms in the [glossary](https://gloss.midicn.com/en/). Use [Learn music](../../learn/) to connect the basics with a starting path and relevant instrument entries. A guide does not replace instrument-specific practice.
