乐理学习可以从一个实际问题开始：读懂谱上的记号、说明一段音乐怎样组织，或把一种写法用到自己的演奏与创作中。

## 读谱与节奏

先识别谱表、音名、时值和拍号，再用短句把符号与声音对应起来。

- [乐理与和声](https://theo.midicn.com/)：按分类阅读读谱、节奏、音程与音阶。
- [音乐词典](https://gloss.midicn.com/)：查记号和术语，沿链接继续读。
- [拍号练习](../../tools/rhythm-metre/)：比较相同速度下不同拍号的重音组织。

读到一个新记号时，找出它影响的是音高、时值、力度还是演奏方式，再在一个短乐句中试用。

## 音阶与和声

- [五度圈](../../tools/circle-of-fifths/)：查调号并比较相邻调。
- [调式](../../tools/scale-modes/)：保持同一主音，比较不同音级；也可保持音列，改变主音。
- [和声进行](../../tools/progression-basic/)与[终止式](../../tools/progression-cadence/)：听和弦连接怎样配合乐句结束。

例如比较 C 大调与 C 自然小调时，先列出两组音，再找出 E/E♭、A/A♭、B/B♭ 的差别。音名、听感和谱面应能相互对应。

## 把知识用到曲子里

选择一段熟悉的旋律，只研究一个问题：句尾落在哪个音、低音怎样变化，或同一节奏是否再次出现。把观察写成一两句话，再对照谱例核对，不必一次分析整首作品。

[学音乐](../../learn/)提供按目标组织的入口；想了解各分站的内容，查看[站点与内容导航](../hub-sites-map/)。

Music theory can begin with a practical question: reading a symbol, explaining how a passage is organised, or using a technique in performance and composition.

## Notation and rhythm

Connect clefs, note names, durations and metre with short musical phrases.

- [Theory](https://theo.midicn.com/en/): browse notation, rhythm, intervals and scales.
- [Glossary](https://gloss.midicn.com/en/): look up a term and follow related explanations.
- [Metre exercise](../../tools/rhythm-metre/): compare the organisation of accents in different metres at the same tempo.

For a new marking, identify whether it concerns pitch, duration, dynamics or technique, then try it within a short phrase.

## Scales and harmony

- [Circle of fifths](../../tools/circle-of-fifths/): find key signatures and compare neighbouring keys.
- [Modes](../../tools/scale-modes/): keep the tonic fixed while changing degrees, or keep the pitch collection while changing the tonic.
- [Progressions](../../tools/progression-basic/) and [cadences](../../tools/progression-cadence/): hear how chord connections support phrase endings.

For example, write C major and C natural minor side by side and locate E/E♭, A/A♭ and B/B♭. Note names, notation and listening should describe the same difference.

## Working with a piece

Choose one question in a familiar melody: its ending note, a change of bass, or a returning rhythm. Write a sentence or two about what you notice and check the score. There is no need to analyse the whole work at once.

[Learn music](../../learn/) groups starting points by goal. The [site and content guide](../hub-sites-map/) explains what each MIDI Music site contains.
