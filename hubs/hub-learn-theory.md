学乐理最常见的失败方式，是把它当成**要背的名词表**。它其实是三层东西，**每层的学法不一样**。

## 你在这里想做什么

先确认你要的是哪一层：

| 你想解决的 | 属于哪一层 | 该去哪 |
|---|---|---|
| 「谱子上这个记号是什么」 | **记号层** | 站外 theo + 词典 |
| 「为什么这里是升号不是降号」 | **原理层** | theo 的调号与和声部分 |
| 「我知道了，然后呢」 | **用法层** | 这里的通识与工具 |

## 去哪读：按三层分

### 第一层：记号层（怎么记）

这一层的核心是**查**，不是学。

- 站外：[音乐理论站](https://theo.midicn.com/) —— 一百五十余条，从音名与谱表开始
- 站外：[音乐词典](https://gloss.midicn.com/) —— 术语速查（**但内容量不足，见下**）
- 站内：[拍号：3/4 与 4/4 听起来为什么不一样](../../tools/rhythm-metre/) —— 拍号这一层的最直观解释

### 第二层：原理层（为什么成立）

这一层是乐理里唯一真正需要"理解"的部分。

- 站外：[音乐理论站](https://theo.midicn.com/) —— 调号、音程、和弦进行那几章
- 站内：[五度圈：十二个调的地图](../../tools/circle-of-fifths/) —— 调号体系的可交互地图
- 站内：[调式：同一个骨架，七种落脚点](../../tools/scale-modes/) —— 为什么同一个音列可以有七种色彩

### 第三层：用法层（怎么用）

这一层决定你能不能把知识变成音乐。

- 站内：[三个最常见的和声进行](../../tools/progression-basic/) —— 最省力的三种
- 站内：[终止式：一句「话说完」的感觉从哪来](../../tools/progression-cadence/) —— 为什么有的结尾"停得住"
- 站内：[挂留与变化音](../../tools/chord-suspension/) —— "还没解决"的那一秒
- 站内：[民歌的变体：没有「原版」这回事](../../culture/folk-variants/) —— 原理层在民歌里怎么松动

### 一条贯穿三层的线

如果你想一次看到"记号 → 原理 → 用法"是怎么连起来的，从[大调为什么听起来快乐](../../culture/major-happy-why/)开始——它从最简单的问题出发，一层层往上走，而且连解释的争议都写了。

## 还没建好的

诚实说三处：

1. 词典站内容量不足 —— 记号层最需要"随手查"，而它还是骨架。这是本条路线最实际的障碍
2. theo 站的乐理部分没有"学习路径" —— 它是 150 条并列的条目，没有标出"先读哪十条"
3. 缺一个"术语之间怎么互相推导"的入口 —— 目前每条都讲自己，不讲邻居

第 2 点我认为是真缺口：150 条并列的知识站，对已经懂的人很好，对新手等于没有顺序。

The most common way to fail at music theory is to treat it as **a list of terms to memorise**. It is really three layers, and each is learned differently.

## What you are trying to do here

First identify which layer you are after:

| What you need | Layer | Where |
|---|---|---|
| "what is this mark on the page" | **notation** | theo + glossary |
| "why a sharp here and not a flat" | **principle** | theo's key-signature and harmony parts |
| "I know it — now what" | **usage** | the culture pages and tools |

## Where to read: by layer

### Layer one: notation (how to read it)

The core of this layer is **lookup**, not study.

- Offsite: [music theory](https://theo.midicn.com/) — over 150 entries, starting from note names and the staff
- Offsite: [glossary](https://gloss.midicn.com/) — term lookup (**but short of its plan, see below**)
- Onsite: [metre: why 3/4 and 4/4 sound different](../../tools/rhythm-metre/) — the most intuitive explanation of this layer

### Layer two: principle (why it holds)

This is the only part of theory that genuinely requires understanding.

- Offsite: [music theory](https://theo.midicn.com/) — the chapters on key signatures, intervals and progressions
- Onsite: [the circle of fifths](../../tools/circle-of-fifths/) — an interactive map of the key-signature system
- Onsite: [modes: one frame, seven home bases](../../tools/scale-modes/) — why one set of pitches carries seven colours

### Layer three: usage (how to apply it)

This layer is what turns knowledge into music.

- Onsite: [the three commonest progressions](../../tools/progression-basic/) — the three cheapest
- Onsite: [cadences: where the feeling of stopping comes from](../../tools/progression-cadence/) — why some endings "land"
- Onsite: [suspensions](../../tools/chord-suspension/) — the second before it resolves
- Onsite: [folk variants: there is no "original"](../../culture/folk-variants/) — where principle loosens in folk song

### One line through all three layers

If you want to see notation → principle → usage connected in one place, **start with [why the major scale sounds happy](../../culture/major-happy-why/)**. It starts from the simplest question, climbs one layer at a time, and includes the dispute about its explanation.

## What is not built yet

Honestly, three gaps:

1. The glossary is short of its plan — lookup is what this layer needs most, and it is still a skeleton. This is the most practical obstacle on this route
2. The theory site has no learning path — it is 150 parallel entries with no indication of "read these ten first"
3. No entry point showing how the terms derive from each other — each entry explains itself and not its neighbours

I consider point two a genuine gap: a 150-entry parallel knowledge site is fine for those who already understand the subject, and equivalent to no order at all for a beginner.
