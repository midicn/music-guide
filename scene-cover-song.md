::: zh
翻唱不是把原曲重录一遍。**你要交的是"你的版本"**，法律上则要分清：词曲是别人的，这一次录音是你的。

## 你要交的是什么

一版能发布的录音：人声、伴奏、混音，加一份说明词曲作者与许可来源的文案。平台规则和地区不同，发布前按平台的实际要求核对，别拿"大家都在传"当依据。

## 三步走

1. 先定调与编曲：改配器、改速度、改段落顺序，让这一版有存在的理由。
2. 录人声与伴奏，人声单独一轨，保留未修音的原轨做对照。
3. 混音与响度对齐平台目标，不要拿原曲母带当响度参考。

## 会用到的页面与工具

- [翻唱与复制](../culture/cover-vs-copy/) —— 先弄清翻唱、改编、盗版三件事的边界。
- [人声处理链](https://prod.midicn.com/vocal-chain/) —— 先判断问题再加环节，链条越长字头越钝。
- [音源分离工具](https://ai.midicn.com/stem-separation-tools/) —— 做伴奏参考可以，直接当发行伴奏要慎重。
- [音高修正](https://prod.midicn.com/pitch-correction/) —— 修的不只是音准，还有滑音、颤音与共振峰。
- [母带交付格式](https://prod.midicn.com/master-delivery-formats/) —— 格式、采样率、位深与真峰值余量一次写清。

## 验收标准

- 单声道检查不塌陷，人声与伴奏无相位抵消。
- 响度约 −14 LUFS，真峰值不高于 −1 dBTP。
- 发布页标注原作者、词曲信息与许可类型，能一句话说清这一版改了什么。
:::

::: en
A cover is not a re-recording of the original. **You are delivering your version**, and legally you must separate two things: the song belongs to someone else, this recording belongs to you.

## What you are delivering

A releasable recording — vocal, backing, mix — plus notes naming the writers and the licence you relied on. Rules differ by platform and territory; check the platform's actual requirements before release instead of trusting that "everyone uploads these".

## Three steps

1. Decide key and arrangement first: change instrumentation, tempo or section order so this version has a reason to exist.
2. Record vocal and backing with the vocal on its own track, and keep the un-tuned take for comparison.
3. Mix to the platform's loudness target instead of copying the original master's level.

## Pages and tools to use

- [Cover versus copy](../culture/cover-vs-copy/) — the boundaries between cover, arrangement and piracy.
- [Vocal chain](https://prod.midicn.com/vocal-chain/) — name the problem before adding a stage; longer chains blunt consonants.
- [Stem separation tools](https://ai.midicn.com/stem-separation-tools/) — fine as a reference backing, risky as a released one.
- [Pitch correction](https://prod.midicn.com/pitch-correction/) — it moves glides, vibrato and formants, not just tuning.
- [Master delivery formats](https://prod.midicn.com/master-delivery-formats/) — state format, sample rate, bit depth and true-peak margin once.

## Acceptance checks

- A mono check does not collapse, and vocal and backing do not cancel each other.
- Loudness around −14 LUFS with true peak no higher than −1 dBTP.
- The release page credits writers and licence type, and one sentence says what this version changed.
:::
