## 互动工具

本站的工具区正在陆续上线，会陆续补上这几类：

- **听辨练习** —— 音程、音阶、和弦与节奏的比对与自测。
- **和声演示** —— 和弦与和声进行在页面里直接听。
- **工具小件** —— 节拍器、调音参考这类常用小工具。

所有工具都在浏览器里直接运行：不用安装，也不调用任何第三方服务。每件工具上线后，都会在首页的快速入口里给出链接。

## Interactive tools

This section is being built out. The following kinds of tools will appear here:

- **Ear-training drills** — compare and test yourself on intervals, scales, chords and rhythm.
- **Harmony demos** — hear chords and harmonic progressions right on the page.
- **Small utilities** — a metronome, a tuning reference and similar everyday helpers.

Everything runs in the browser: nothing to install, and no third-party services are called. Each tool gets its own link from the home page once it goes live.
