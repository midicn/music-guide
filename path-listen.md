::: zh
「听了很多年，却说不清好在哪。」这条路径不改手上的功夫，改的是判断力：知道一首曲子由什么搭起来，该在哪个位置竖起耳朵。

## 这条路径适合谁

适合每周听歌超过五小时、却不满足于「好听 / 不好听」的人；也适合正在学乐器、想给练琴补上判断的人。不适合想马上弹出声音的人——那是[学一件乐器](../path-instrument/)。

## 先读这三页

- [曲式](https://theo.midicn.com/form/) —— 重复、对比、回归是三种组织动作，先认这三个词，整首曲子就有骨架可抓。
- [体裁与曲式辨异](https://theo.midicn.com/genre-vs-form/) —— 体裁问「做什么用」，曲式问「怎么搭」，混用这两个词，后面每一页都会读得别扭。
- [巴洛克音乐](https://hist.midicn.com/baroque/) —— 拿一个时期当样本：通奏低音、协奏曲、赋格都在这一页落地，听什么就有了具体对象。

## 接下来

- [练耳的意义与方法](https://theo.midicn.com/ear-training/) —— 把「听到了」变成「说得出」。
- [奏鸣曲式](https://theo.midicn.com/sonata-form/) —— 古典与浪漫的长曲大多靠它组织。
- [拍号：3/4 与 4/4 听起来为什么不一样](../tools/rhythm-metre/) —— 可以点着听，是成本最低的听觉训练。
- [听辨枢纽](../hubs/hub-listen-better/) —— 按主题继续挑，不必按站的顺序读。

## 可以跳过

记谱软件、DAW 与混音（走[修复、混音与交付](../path-finish/)时再看）；和声写作与声部进行的规则；乐器练习法。现在分散注意力最不划算——你要练的是「指出具体现象」，不是术语量。

## 常见走弯路

1. 先背术语再去听。顺序反了：术语是用来命名你已经听见的东西。
2. 只听推荐的「名曲」，不听自己熟的歌。熟的歌才有对照。
3. 把「听懂」当成能报出曲式名称。真正的标志是能预判下一句往哪走。

::: en
You have listened for years and still cannot say why a piece works. This path changes your judgement, not your hands: knowing what a piece is built from, and where to prick up your ears.

## Who this path is for

For anyone who listens more than five hours a week and is tired of "I like it / I don't"; also for players who want judgement alongside practice. Not for anyone who wants sound under their fingers tomorrow — that is [Learning an Instrument](../path-instrument/).

## Read these three first

- [Musical Form](https://theo.midicn.com/form/) — repetition, contrast and return are the three organising moves; learn these words and a whole piece gains a skeleton.
- [Genre versus Form](https://theo.midicn.com/genre-vs-form/) — genre asks what a piece is for, form asks how it is built; confuse them and every later page reads wrong.
- [Baroque Music](https://hist.midicn.com/baroque/) — use one period as a sample: continuo, concerto and fugue all land here, so "what to listen for" gets a concrete object.

## What comes next

- [Ear Training](https://theo.midicn.com/ear-training/) — turn "I heard it" into "I can say it".
- [Sonata Form](https://theo.midicn.com/sonata-form/) — how most long Classical and Romantic works are organised.
- [Metre: why 3/4 and 4/4 sound different](../tools/rhythm-metre/) — clickable, and the cheapest ear training there is.
- [Listening hub](../hubs/hub-listen-better/) — keep choosing by topic rather than by site.

## What you can skip

Notation software, DAWs and mixing (save them for [fix, mix and deliver](../path-finish/)); the rules of harmony writing and voice leading; instrument practice routines. Nothing scatters attention faster right now — the skill is pointing at a concrete event, not collecting terms.

## Common detours

1. Memorising terms before listening. That order is backwards: terms name what you already hear.
2. Only playing the recommended canon, never your own familiar songs. Familiar songs give you a control group.
3. Treating "understanding" as naming the form. The real sign is predicting where the next phrase goes.
