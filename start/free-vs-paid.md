音乐学习里的「免费」与「付费」，不是好坏之分，是**成本结构**之分。看清三笔隐藏账，你就能自己回答「要不要花这笔钱」。

## 免费的隐藏成本

1. **时间成本**：免费资源常常是"零件"——教程是散的、路径要你自己拼。省下的钱，可能以"摸索三个月"的形式还回去。
2. **注意力成本**：广告与推荐流不是白来的，它们就是免费产品的收入。练琴这种需要深度专注的事，最怕这个。
3. **不稳定的成本**：免费内容随时可能消失、改版、断更——你越依赖它，风险越大。

## 付费买到的三样东西

- **结构**：成体系的课程帮你省掉"自己拼图"，把路径直接摆出来；
- **诊断**：老师的眼睛和耳朵——**这是音乐学习里最值得付的一笔钱**，因为机器与教程都做不到"看你个人的问题";
- **承诺**：付了钱，你更不容易放弃。这不是伟光正的话术，是行为规律——沉没成本会替你做一部分坚持。

## 花钱的优先级（一份参考）

1. **一件可靠的乐器**（见「买什么乐器」——不必贵的，但不能差）；
2. **一段时间的好老师**（哪怕一个月一次）；
3. **工具与软件**（按需，见「音乐软件怎么选」）；
4. 其余的一切，几乎都可以后置。

## 关于"白嫖"的几句实话

完全免费的路径真实存在——公共版权乐谱、开放数据集、公共课、开源软件。它们**足以支撑一个自律的初学者走很远**。付费解决的从来不是"能不能"，而是"快不快、稳不稳、少不少绕路"。

"Free" vs "paid" in music learning is not about quality — it is about **cost structure**. Read three hidden bills and you can answer "should I pay?" yourself.

## The hidden costs of free

1. **Time**: free resources often arrive as parts — scattered tutorials, a path you assemble yourself. The money saved may be repaid as three months of wandering.
2. **Attention**: ads and feeds are not free; they are the product's income. Deep-focus work like practice suffers most.
3. **Stability**: free content can vanish, change or go stale — the more you depend on it, the bigger the risk.

## What paying actually buys

- **Structure**: a systematic course removes the assembly work and lays the path out;
- **Diagnosis**: a teacher's eyes and ears — **the single most worthwhile purchase in music**, because no machine or tutorial can see *your* specific problem;
- **Commitment**: money makes quitting harder. Not a slogan — sunk cost quietly does part of the persisting for you.

## A priority order for spending

1. **One reliable instrument** (see "What Instrument Does My Budget Buy" — not expensive, but not bad);
2. **A stretch with a good teacher** (even monthly);
3. **Tools and software** (as needed, see "Choosing Music Software");
4. Everything else can wait.

## A few honest words about "free-riding"

A fully free path really exists — public-domain scores, open datasets, open courses, open-source software. It can carry a disciplined beginner very far. Paying never answers "can I?" — it answers "faster, steadier, fewer detours".
