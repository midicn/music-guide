选择吉他时，先明确想演奏的声音，再检查乐器的尺寸、弦距和音准。钢弦、尼龙弦与电吉他都可以系统学习，不能用“正统”与“只扫弦”区分。

## 三类吉他的使用条件

| 类型 | 先确认什么 |
|---|---|
| 钢弦木吉他 | 你是否喜欢钢弦音色；琴体尺寸与按弦手感是否合适 |
| 古典吉他 | 尼龙弦音色、较宽指板与所学曲目是否符合目标 |
| 电吉他 | 除琴之外，是否有可用的音箱、音频接口或耳机监听方案 |

同一类琴也可能有不同弦长、琴颈与琴体。尽量试弹具体型号，不只按照片、价格或“入门款”标签决定。

## 试琴时做的检查

请店家先调准弦，再检查空弦和几个常用把位是否发音清楚。按弦力度、弦距、琴颈状态与技巧都会影响手感；初学者按不好 F 大横按，不能单凭这一点判定琴有问题。

第十二品实音与同弦泛音可用于检查八度音准，但按弦过重也会把音推高。发现明显差异时，让有经验的教师或维修人员进一步检查。二手琴还应确认琴颈、琴桥、品丝以及电吉他的线路状态。

## 第一次练习

先调弦，练习几个单音发声，再学习所需和弦或短旋律。一个 C 大三和弦可以帮助认识和弦构成，但不能据此伴奏所有歌曲；还要学习节奏、换和弦和具体曲目的和声。

::: en
Choose the sound you want to play, then check size, setup and intonation. Steel-string acoustic, classical and electric guitars all support systematic study; none is inherently the only serious starting point.

## Practical differences

| Type | What to establish first |
|---|---|
| Steel-string acoustic | Whether you like its sound and find the body size and setup comfortable |
| Classical | Whether nylon-string sound, the wider fingerboard and repertoire suit your goals |
| Electric | What amplifier, audio-interface or headphone arrangement you will use alongside the guitar |

Scale length, neck shape and body size also vary within each type. Try the actual instrument rather than deciding from pictures, price or a beginner label alone.

## Trying an instrument

Ask for it to be tuned, then check clear notes on open strings and several common positions. Technique, string height, neck condition and fretting pressure all affect feel. Difficulty with an F barre chord alone does not diagnose a faulty instrument.

The fretted twelfth-fret note and the same string's harmonic can help check octave intonation, although excessive pressure can sharpen a fretted note. Have a teacher or repairer assess an obvious discrepancy. For a used instrument, also check neck, bridge and frets, and electrical components where present.

## Beginning practice

Tune first, work on a few clear notes, then learn chords or a short melody appropriate to the repertoire. A C major triad illustrates chord construction; it is not sufficient accompaniment for every song. Rhythm, chord changes and the actual progression also matter.
