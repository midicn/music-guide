乐理可以与正在听、弹或写的音乐一起学。先选择一段短音乐，再决定目前需要解决的是音高、节奏、和弦，还是段落组织，不必按同一套顺序学完所有概念。

## 从音高与节奏建立对应

用琴键或谱面找到一个音，再听相邻音的距离。以 C 大调为例，C–D–E–F–G–A–B–C 的相邻距离是全、全、半、全、全、全、半。E–F 与 B–C 是半音关系；白键之间并非距离都相同。

```

同时练习读时值与保持拍点。音高和节奏可以先分开检查，再合在同一短句里；如果目标是读谱，就不必因为还没完成听辨课程而暂停。

## 逐步扩大分析范围

1. **音程与音阶**：写出音名，再比较距离、主音与调号。
2. **和弦**：列出构成音，分别听单音与和弦整体。
3. **和声进行**：跟随低音和和弦变化，观察句尾怎样收束。
4. **曲式**：标记材料的重复、对比与返回，不只背结构名称。

例如在《D大调卡农》中，可以先辨认低音循环，再观察上方声部怎样变化；它能说明某种写法，不代表所有作品都按同样的循环组织。

## 用一个问题检验理解

读完概念后，试着写出一个自己的例子，并解释它满足了哪些条件。学大三和弦，可从 C–E–G 开始，再改成 F–A–C；若只会背“大三度加小三度”，还需要把距离落实到音名或谱面。

继续阅读 [[concept:pitch|音高]]、[[concept:major-scale|大调音阶]]与 [[concept:chord|和弦]]，或从主站[学音乐](../../learn/)按目标选择路径。

::: en
Study theory alongside music you listen to, play or write. Choose a short passage, then decide whether the immediate question concerns pitch, rhythm, chords or form. Every learner need not finish the same sequence first.

## Connecting notation and sound

Locate a note on a keyboard or score and hear its distance from adjacent notes. In C major, C–D–E–F–G–A–B–C follows whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half steps. E–F and B–C are semitones; adjacent white keys are not all equally far apart.

```

Also practise reading durations and maintaining a pulse. Pitch and rhythm may be checked separately before being combined in one phrase. A notation goal need not wait until a complete ear-training course is finished.

## Expanding the analysis

1. **Intervals and scales**: write note names and compare distances, tonic and key signature.
2. **Chords**: list their tones and hear them individually and together.
3. **Progressions**: follow bass and chord changes, particularly at phrase endings.
4. **Form**: mark repetition, contrast and return rather than memorising names alone.

In Pachelbel's Canon in D, for example, first recognise the bass cycle and then follow changes above it. The piece illustrates one procedure, not a universal structure for all music.

## Checking understanding

Write an example of your own and explain its conditions. For a major triad, move from C–E–G to F–A–C and identify the intervals. Remembering “a major third plus a minor third” still needs to be connected to pitches or notation.
