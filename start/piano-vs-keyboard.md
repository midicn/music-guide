「买钢琴还是买键盘？」——这问题的答案，先取决于你想要的其实是**哪一件东西**。它们名字相近，设计目标完全不同。

## 三件东西，三种设计

| | 声学钢琴 | 数码钢琴 | 合成器/编曲键盘 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **发声** | 琴槌敲弦，物理共鸣 | 采样/建模回放 | 电子合成 |
| **键盘** | 真实机械配重 | 配重（逐代接近真琴） | 多为半配重或无配重 |
| **音量** | 无法静音 | 可戴耳机 | 可戴耳机 |
| **维护** | 调律、防潮、搬运难 | 几乎免维护 | 免维护 |
| **核心场景** | 古典训练、舞台 | 家庭学琴主力 | 编曲、乐队、音色实验 |

## 先回答三个问题

1. **你学的是「钢琴」还是「键盘」？** 想弹古典曲目、要练触键——选配重键盘（真钢或电钢）；想做编曲、玩音色——键盘更合适。
2. **你的空间和邻居是什么局面？** 公寓 + 晚练 → 电钢（耳机）几乎是唯一解。
3. **五年内你会搬家吗？** 声学钢琴的搬运成本很高，电钢可以背着走。

## 一个务实的建议

预算有限、方向未定：**先买电钢**。它的手感足以支撑到中高级的绝大部分内容，还附带静音与便携。等你确定「这辈子要跟古典钢琴死磕」或者「家里有稳定的空间」，再考虑声学钢琴——那时你会买得更准。

## 关于「手感」的一个实话

手感是键盘乐器的核心参数，而它**只能用手测，不能看参数**。去琴行，弹三种：配重的电钢、真钢琴、无配重的键盘——五分钟你就有答案。

## 听一听：相邻的两个键，是音乐最小的距离

键盘乐器的布局本身就是乐理图：相邻两个键之间就是最小的距离——半音。听听这两个「隔壁邻居」合起来的样子，这就是键盘帮所有人理解音乐的方式。

"Piano or keyboard?" The answer depends first on **which thing you actually want**. The names sit close together; the design goals do not.

## Three things, three designs

| | Acoustic piano | Digital piano | Synth / arranger keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sound** | Hammers strike strings, physical resonance | Sampled or modelled playback | Electronic synthesis |
| **Keys** | Real mechanical action | Weighted (ever closer to real) | Mostly semi- or unweighted |
| **Volume** | Cannot be silenced | Headphones | Headphones |
| **Upkeep** | Tuning, humidity, hard to move | Near zero | Zero |
| **Core scene** | Classical training, stage | The home practice workhorse | Arranging, bands, sound design |

## Answer three questions first

1. **Are you learning "piano" or "keyboard"?** Classical repertoire and touch need weighted keys (acoustic or digital); arranging and sound design prefer a keyboard.
2. **What are your space and neighbours like?** Flat plus late hours — a digital with headphones is almost the only answer.
3. **Will you move within five years?** Moving an acoustic piano is expensive; a digital rides along.

## One pragmatic suggestion

Limited budget, undecided direction: **start with a digital piano**. Its action carries you through the vast majority of intermediate material, with silence and portability included. Once you are sure "I will wrestle classical piano for life" or "I have a stable room", consider an acoustic — you will buy more accurately then.

## One truth about "action"

Action is the core spec of any keyboard instrument, and it **can only be measured by hand, not read from a spec sheet**. Go to a shop and play three: a weighted digital, an acoustic, an unweighted keyboard — five minutes gives you your answer.

## Listen: two neighbouring keys, music's smallest distance

A keyboard's layout is itself a theory diagram: neighbouring keys sit one semitone apart. Hear what those two neighbours sound like together — this is how the keyboard helps everyone understand music.
