练习时长取决于目标、经验、注意力和演奏任务。先安排一个能持续执行的时段，再记录实际完成的内容，比采用所有人通用的分钟数更有用。

## 一个二十分钟的安排示例

这是一种可调整的分配，不是固定处方：

| 时间 | 内容 | 结束时检查 |
|---|---|---|
| 3 分钟 | 熟悉的发声或基本动作 | 是否能稳定开始，而非立即追求速度 |
| 10 分钟 | 一处明确困难，如两小节的换指或节奏 | 哪次成功，失败发生在哪里 |
| 5 分钟 | 放回前后乐句中演奏 | 单独解决的问题能否带回音乐 |
| 2 分钟 | 记录与下次安排 | 记下速度、片段和仍需解决的问题 |

若可用时间更短，可以只处理一个片段；较长时段则拆成不同任务并安排休息。没有完成计划时，记录原因即可，不必用加倍练习补偿。

## 怎样判断有没有练到目标

把“弹完一遍”与“改善一个问题”分开记录。完整演奏可以检查连贯和记忆；分段、变速与改变节奏则可用于定位问题。重复十遍也不保证有效，要知道每遍在比较什么。

例如先在能稳定完成的速度弹两遍，再略微加速。如果错误集中在同一处，回到该处连同前后各一小段练习，而不是只从开头重新开始。

## 记录的最小内容

日期、片段、速度或任务、一个观察、下一次动作，已经足够。几天后比较记录，决定继续、换方法或向教师请教。更多方法见 [[concept:practice-method|练习方法]]与 [[concept:slow-practice|慢练]]。

Practice duration depends on goals, experience, concentration and the playing task. Begin with a manageable time slot and record what was achieved rather than adopting a universal number of minutes.

## An adjustable twenty-minute example

| Time | Task | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| 3 minutes | Familiar tone production or basic movement | A steady start rather than immediate speed |
| 10 minutes | One defined problem, such as fingering or rhythm in two bars | Where attempts succeed or fail |
| 5 minutes | Return to the surrounding phrase | Whether the isolated improvement transfers |
| 2 minutes | Record progress and the next task | Passage, tempo and unresolved difficulty |

A shorter session may address one passage. Divide longer sessions into tasks with breaks. If the plan was not completed, note why; there is no need to repay it with double practice.

## Testing the goal

Record a complete play-through separately from improvement of one problem. A play-through can check continuity and memory; changes of section, tempo and rhythm can help locate a difficulty. Ten repetitions do not guarantee improvement unless each has a purpose.

For example, play twice at a reliable tempo, then increase it slightly. If the same spot fails, work on it with a little surrounding context rather than repeatedly restarting the whole piece.

The 60 BPM example is a pulse reference. State which note value receives the beat, and choose a speed at which movement and timing remain coordinated.

## A minimal practice record

Date, passage, tempo or task, one observation and the next action are enough. Compare entries after a few days to decide whether to continue, change the method or ask a teacher. See [[concept:practice-method|Practice Methods]] and [[concept:slow-practice|Slow Practice]].
