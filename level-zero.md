## 你现在大概会什么

能听出喜欢与不喜欢，能跟着歌哼，但说不出为什么；多数时候不识谱，手上没有乐器，也没进过排练房。这不是短板清单，是起点。

## 最该补的一件事

建立「听什么」的词汇：能在歌里指出一个具体现象——鼓点在哪、副歌什么时候进、和弦换了没有。术语量不重要，能指认才重要。这一层不通，买琴、报课、下练习 App 都只是在换入口。

## 三页起步

- [从零开始学音乐，第一步做什么](../start/first-steps/) —— 先定方向，比先买东西重要。
- [拍号：3/4 与 4/4 听起来为什么不一样](../tools/rhythm-metre/) —— 能点着听，是最快上手的听觉抓手。
- [练耳的意义与方法](https://theo.midicn.com/ear-training/) —— 练耳不是听音名，是建立「听到就知道」的通道。

## 暂时别碰

和声学与声部进行、配器、混音与母带、AI 工具选型、考级路线。它们都建立在「听得出差别」之上，现在读只会变成背诵。

## 怎么判断自己进阶了

- 跟着 4/4 数拍，一分钟不丢。
- 听一遍就能指出副歌从第几句进。
- 同一首曲子的两个版本，能说出三处具体差别。

## What you can probably do now

You can tell like from dislike, you can hum along, but you cannot say why. Usually you do not read notation, own no instrument and have never been in a rehearsal room. This is a starting point, not a list of faults. The good news: this layer needs repetitions, not talent.

## The one thing to fix

Build a vocabulary for what to listen for: point at one concrete event in a song — where the drums sit, when the chorus enters, whether the chords changed. The number of terms is irrelevant; pointing is the skill. Without this layer, a new instrument, a course or a practice app is just another entrance.

## Three pages to start

- [First steps from zero](../start/first-steps/) — setting a direction matters more than buying something.
- [Metre: why 3/4 and 4/4 sound different](../tools/rhythm-metre/) — clickable, and the fastest handle for the ear.
- [Ear Training](https://theo.midicn.com/ear-training/) — not naming notes, but building a channel from hearing to knowing.

## Do not touch yet

Harmony and voice leading, orchestration, mixing and mastering, AI tool selection, graded exam routes. All of them rest on hearing differences; read now and they turn into memorisation.

## How you know you have moved up

- You can count 4/4 for a minute without losing the beat.
- One listen is enough to point at the line where the chorus enters.
- You can name three concrete differences between two versions of the same piece.
